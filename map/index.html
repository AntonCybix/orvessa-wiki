    Map

    The world map of Orvessa, drawn from the game's own map data.

    The world map is drawn from the same data as the game's map: zones, subzones, settlements, landmarks, dungeons, ship and boat routes, and the 512 m block grid. Hover to see the position in metres and its block (block x_y); click a zone, subzone, settlement or dungeon to open its page.

    Drag to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom, 0 for the whole world. Click a zone, subzone, settlement or dungeon to open its page.
