    Citadel Guard is a creature of Marrowfield (level 36–38).

    In the game

    In the game the Citadel Guard is level 36–38, normal. It is found in The Marrow Citadel. It can drop Picket Pauldrons (2%), Veteran's Hauberk (2%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: The Citadel Guard (quest) (behind the level cap).
