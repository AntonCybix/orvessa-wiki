    Reckoning of years

    The peoples of Orvessa count from the night Varnhalt chained Ezrakhar: the year of the Vigil.

    The peoples of Orvessa count from the night Varnhalt chained Ezrakhar: the year of the Vigil. The Ember Year, when the game begins, is the 3,412th year of the Vigil. Each faction keeps its own calendar besides:

    Description

    • Ironreach counts the reigns of its emperors.
    • The Covenant counts the years since the fall of old Edrath, and calls them years of the Long Night.
    • The Wildwood counts seasons, not years.
    • The Syndicate counts nothing it cannot sell, but dates its contracts by the Vigil like everyone else.

    Some in Kaelmor have begun to call this year the first year after the Wall. All numbers and calendar names are working proposals.
