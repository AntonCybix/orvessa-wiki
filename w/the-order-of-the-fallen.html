    The Order of the Fallen
    Organisation

    The one order in Orvessa that still honours the dead gods. Its priests keep godsbone relics, lay the restless dead to rest and try to free the wraiths the Covenant binds, and they deny the emperors of Varr any right to Aurelan's mantle. Its oldest chapter is in Kaelmor, where its brothers and sisters kept Varnhalt's hall in the centuries when he grew too weak to climb his own stairs. They fed him, read to him, and kept the door to the vault. On the night he died they sealed his hall with his own rune and have let no one in since, not even the Syndicate's council. They will not say what they saw. Some say they are grieving. Others say they are hiding something. Both are true.
