    Grimfur Wolf is a creature of Grimbough Forest (level 36–37).

    In the game

    In the game the Grimfur Wolf is level 36–37, normal. It is found in The Wolfways and The Hearth-Huts. It can drop rarely one of 12 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: Wolves on the North Track (behind the level cap) and The Pack-Trails (behind the level cap).
