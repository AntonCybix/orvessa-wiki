    Terms for the Tower is a level 17 chain quest of Milecairn, step 6 of 6 of Milecairn tollbreakers chain (one of 2 branches at that step: a player takes only one). Bring the leader's terms to the toll warden at the east tower.

    The quest

    The Ridge Watcher says: “Here are his terms as his runner gave them, written out in my hand. The toll warden at the east tower speaks for the wardens on this side of the field. Take them to him and let him decide. I would rather a warden said no than a watcher.”

    Objectives

    • Bring the Leader's Terms to the Toll Warden

    Where

    It is given by the Ridge Watcher, in Tollbreak Ridge, and it is handed in to the Toll Warden, by the high toll. It is offered only to a hero who chose “Carry his terms to the toll warden at the east tower” in The Leader's Word.

    Rewards

    850 XP at its level and 14 coin.

    Completion

    “Halve the toll and look the other way. And they did not open the cairns. I believe that last part, which is more than I can say for the rest. I will send it west to the captain with my own word on it. Until he answers, nobody on this road fights who does not have to.”

    In the chain

    Before it: The Leader's Word. It ends the chain.
