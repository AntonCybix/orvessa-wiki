    Highway Slinger is a creature of Port Ossery (level 17–18).

    In the game

    In the game the Highway Slinger is level 17–18, normal. It is found in The Bleak Downs. It can drop Deckhand's Belt (1%), Net-Hauler's Gloves (1%), Tarred Bracers (1%), Wharf Boots (1%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.
