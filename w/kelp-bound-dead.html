    Kelp-Bound Dead is a creature of The Shrouded Coast (level 21–23).

    In the game

    In the game the Kelp-Bound Dead is level 21–23, normal. It is undead. It is found in The Far Spit and The Wreck Strand. It can drop Fog-Grey Sash (1%), Marram-Bound Bracers (1%), Salt-Stiff Cloak (1%), Shingle-Treader Boots (1%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: The Overrun Light.
