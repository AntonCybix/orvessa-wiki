    In the game

    In the game the Dry-Tide Eel is level 45–48, normal. It is found in The White Flats. It can drop Crawler-Plate Greaves (1%), Eel-Skin Jerkin (1%), Flats-Walker's Boots (1%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: Eels in the Pans (behind the level cap), Eels round the Posts (behind the level cap) and Eels on the South Flats (behind the level cap).
