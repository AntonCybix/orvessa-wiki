    Adders in the Carr is a level 12 side quest of Lanternfen. Kill 4 Bog-Adders at the carr's edge west of the lantern-mender, then return to the lantern-mender.

    The quest

    The Lantern-Mender says: “The bog-adders lie along the carr's edge west of my fire, right where I walk to my poles. They do not move for a boot. They bite it. Clear four of them out, and I can do my rounds without counting my toes after.”

    Objectives

    • Kill: Bog-Adder ×4

    Where

    It is given by the Lantern-Mender, and it is handed in to the Lantern-Mender.

    Rewards

    775 XP at its level and 8 coin.

    Completion

    “Four fewer on the path. The rest will learn to lie somewhere else.”
