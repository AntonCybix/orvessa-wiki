    Heather for the Mead is a level 11 side quest of The Kindling Hills. Gather 6 handfuls of flowering heather on the west heath top and bring them to the inn host at the crossroads.

    The quest

    The Heath Watcher says: “The inn host brews the mead with flowering heather from this hill, and nobody else knows where the best of it grows. I do: on the east side of the heath top. Gather six good handfuls and carry them down to the inn.”

    Objectives

    • Gather: Flowering Heather (6)

    Where

    It is given by the Heath Watcher, in The Beacon Heights, and it is handed in to the Roadside Inn Host, in The Three Kettles.

    Rewards

    1075 XP at its level, 9 coin and Heather Mead ×2.

    Completion

    “From the watcher's hill? That is the good heather. Here, take two of last year's, and come back for this year's when it is ready.”
