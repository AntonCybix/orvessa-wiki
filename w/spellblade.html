    A school of every power, founded by soldiers who carried a sliver of a fallen god's power into their steel.

    Spellbook

    Role: Melee DPS / Off-tank (hybrid) · Resource: Mana + Arc Charge (built by melee strikes, spent on spells) · Races: Dwarves, Forged, The Risen, Bloodkin, Sylvani, Corsairs

    A warrior who soaks the blade in pure elemental magic. Fast physical attacks combined with magic strikes at close range.

    In Orvessa: the old spellblade orders trained to fight demons hand to hand during the war of the gods. Their schools are half-empty now, but the Deep is giving them a reason to fill up again.
