    Every people has its own tradition, from Sylvani herb-craft to Goblin bombs and Bloodkin blood-draughts. Alchemists heal, poison and blow things up, depending on who is paying.

    Spellbook

    Role: Support / Healer / DPS · Resource: Reagents (Vials) + Mana; some spells gain bonus effects depending on the vial used · Races: Dwarves, Goblins, The Risen, Bloodkin, Blight-Born, Sylvani, Thornkin, Corsairs, Tide-Born, Half-Bloods

    Throws flasks of healing extract at allies, and corrosive acids, poison bombs and smoke grenades at enemies. Balances the fight with chemical elixirs.

    In Orvessa: alchemy needs no god, only the right ingredients. Ground godsbone heals, distilled ashblood kills, and every alchemist pays dearly for both.

    Talent trees

    • Apothecary (healer): Gentle Brew, Bottled Vitality, Revitalizing Mist, Antidote Mastery. Capstone: Elixir of Life, revives a fallen ally.
    • Toxicologist (poison, DoT): Deadly Concoction, Neurotoxin (slows casting), Venom Stack, Plague Vial. Capstone: Black Mamba Extract, the target dies after 8 s unless cured.
    • Bombardier (explosive DPS): Bigger Bangs, Shrapnel, Napalm Flask, Chain Reaction. Capstone: Nitroglycerin, an enormous AoE with a long cooldown.
