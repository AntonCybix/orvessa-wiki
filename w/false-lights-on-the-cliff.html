    False Lights on the Cliff is a level 21 chain quest of The Shrouded Coast, step 1 of 3 of The Shrouded Coast wreckers chain. Climb to the east cliff's top and look at 3 False Lamps on a Pole along its edge, then return to the cove-watcher.

    The quest

    The Cove-Watcher says: “When a light goes dark, the wreckers hang their own lamps along the cliff's edge. A ship that sees them takes them for the coast's lights and steers onto the rocks. Walk up the slope west of here to the cliff top by the east tower, and look at three of those lamps. I want to know how they are hung.”

    Objectives

    • Examine: False Lamp on a Pole (3)

    Where

    It is given by the Cove-Watcher, in The Far Spit, and it is handed in to the Cove-Watcher, in The Far Spit.

    Rewards

    2325 XP at its level and 22 coin.

    Completion

    “Tarred poles, hooked arms and good glass. Not driftwood work: somebody paid for those. Somebody who knows which nights the coast's lights will be out.”

    In the chain

    It opens the chain. After it: The Cove Crews.
