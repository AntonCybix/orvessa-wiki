    Heavy armour, steam-driven in the garrison halls of Varr or flesh-hardened among the Blight-Born war bands. A Juggernaut gets stronger the more it is hit.

    Spellbook

    Role: Tank (physical) · Resource: Pressure (steam pressure, built by dealing and taking damage, released in spells) · Races: Dwarves, Forged, The Risen, Blight-Born, Thornkin, Beast-Touched, Corsairs, Tide-Born

    Clad in full plate and armed with shield and hammer, or a heavy artillery weapon. Absorbs enormous amounts of damage and controls the battlefield.

    In Orvessa: Varnhalt was the god of the shield wall, and the first Juggernauts were the soldiers who stood beside him. Their steam armour is an Ironreach invention, but the oath to hold the line is older.
