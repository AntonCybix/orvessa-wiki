    Under the Sink

    The one dungeon of Brackwater Verge, under the Sink on the South Flats (levels 8–10).

    Verge has one dungeon. Its way in is the sealed hatch at the bottom of the Sink on the South Flats, where the roofs of a drowned town show at low water. Below lies that town, older than Kaelmor: a town that worshipped what lies below, an early cult of the Deep. Its people made heirlooms of Ezrakhar marked with an eye under a crown, filled with dark magic and usable only by those who can wield it. In its flooded heart the Blackwake keeps its anchorage. At the end the player meets the Physician, who escapes. Names and layout still to be decided.

    In the game

    In the game Under the Sink is an instance for a group: tuned for three heroes of levels 8–10, with room for 5. Each party gets a copy of its own; what is killed stays dead in that copy, and the copy resets 4 hours after it was made.

    The way in is the hatch at the bottom of the sinkhole on the South Flats, forced open by the pit-diggers. It lets in heroes of level 5 or more who hold the dungeon's quest, Under the Sink (taken or done); anyone else reads: “The hatch is broken open, but you have no business down there yet. Ysolde Marrow in Reedstep is looking for people to send.”

    Its areas, bosses and enemies below are the game's; the area and boss names marked proposed are the proposal's chosen names, which wait for the owner's approval.

    Bosses

    The Drowned Prior (proposed name), level 9, in The Kneeling Chapel (proposed name). Abilities: Staff Strike and Kneel.

    • Phase 1, from the pull: uses Kneel and Staff Strike.
    • Phase 2, below 60 % health: uses Kneel and Staff Strike; adds join (3 × Rot-Touched Townsman, again every 25 s).
    • Phase 3, below 25 % health: uses Kneel and Staff Strike; gains Quickened.
    • Loot: one of Nave-Stone Shield, Silt-Crusted Hauberk, Niche-Robber's Gloves, Chorister's Cowl, Drowned Vestment and Verger's Chain; and Old Temple Stone ×2–3; coin.

    Boarding-Master Dace Skerrick (proposed name), level 10, in The Rootbound Anchorage (proposed name). Abilities: Heavy Cut and Grapnel.

    • Phase 1, from the pull: uses Grapnel and Heavy Cut.
    • Phase 2, below 70 % health: uses Grapnel and Heavy Cut; adds join (2 × Drowned Crewman, again every 25 s).
    • Phase 3, below 35 % health (or 90 s into the phase before): uses Grapnel and Heavy Cut; falling rigging strikes parts of the arena.
    • Loot: one of Root-Grown Cuirass, Boarder's Cable Belt, Rope-Climber's Boots, Tide-Soaked Mantle, Drowned Quay Lantern and Cable-Chain Bracers; and Drowned Hull Fittings ×2–4 and Pattern: Hull-Riveted Buckler (20 %); coin.

    The Physician, level 10, in The Draught Hold (proposed name). Abilities: Flung Flask, Rot and Bitter Physic.

    • Phase 1, from the pull: uses Flung Flask and Rot.
    • Phase 2, below 70 % health: uses Bitter Physic, Flung Flask and Rot; adds join (2 × Rot-Touched Townsman, again every 20 s).
    • Phase 3, below 40 % health: uses Bitter Physic, Flung Flask and Rot.
    • At 20 % health he drinks a draught, leaves the fight and escapes: he cannot be killed here. Driving him off completes the encounter, and he leaves The Physician's Satchel behind, which holds his loot.
    • Loot: one of Physician's Lancet, Brewer's Stirring Staff, Vat-Iron Helm, Hold-Plank Greaves, Stained Leather Apron, Stained Handling Gloves, Remedy-Bearer's Cloak and Stoppered Vial; and Clear Spirit ×2–4 and Pattern: Clear Remedy (30 %); coin.
