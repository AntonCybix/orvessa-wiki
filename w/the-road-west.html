    The Road West is a level 18 main quest of Milecairn, step 10 of 10 of Milecairn road chain. Speak to the warden-captain at the west tower, then follow the road west toward the gate.

    The quest

    The Toll Warden says: “You have walked this road from end to end, and you know it better than most of my wardens. Go back west to the warden-captain at the first tower and tell him what you have seen at this end: the opened cairns, the fires, the scholar's letter. Then keep walking. Past his tower the road runs on to the port and the coast, and they will want to hear it there too.”

    Objectives

    • Talk to the Warden-Captain
    • Reach The Road West

    Where

    It is given by the Toll Warden, by the high toll, and it is complete as soon as its last objective is.

    Rewards

    1120 XP at its level, 16 coin and Salt Poultice ×2.

    Completion

    “The gravel runs on west toward the port. Whatever is waking the field, word of it goes with you.”

    In the chain

    Before it: Hold the East Tower. It ends the chain.
