    The Count That Does Not Match is a level 23 hidden quest of The Shrouded Coast. Look at the Stone Cut with Tallies at the third light's foot.

    The quest

    The game says: “A third stone at the third light's foot, cut the same way as the others: rows of four strokes and a fifth across. Somebody counts the boats from every light. But this count is shorter than the east tower's by a full row.”

    Objectives

    • Examine: Stone Cut with Tallies

    It is a hidden quest: no one gives it, and it shows on no map until it is found.

    Where

    It is found by Tallies at the Third Light (found, not given), in The Far Spit, and it is complete as soon as its last objective is.

    Rewards

    2535 XP at its level, 24 coin and Salt Poultice ×2.

    Completion

    “Fewer boats passed the third light than passed the east tower. Between the two lies the mooring. The missing boats never went past the last light at all.”
