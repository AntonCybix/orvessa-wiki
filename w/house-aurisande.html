    Isembard Aurisande. Polished, kind and ice-cold. Wants trade to go on, whoever rules.

    Gold rings on every finger. Taking one off means he is serious.

    Marisol Aurisande, an assessor in the Saltmarket who knows what everything costs

    Lisette Aurisande fled to Avelorn with the guild's debts and sells forged relics there
