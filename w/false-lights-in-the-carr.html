    False Lights in the Carr is a level 16 main quest of Lanternfen, step 7 of 12 of Lanternfen lanterns chain. Kill 4 Marsh-Hags in the carr south of the carr-walker's fire and examine 2 False Lights near them, then go to the carr-walker.

    The quest

    The Candlewife says: “There are lights in the carr at night, south of here between the flats and the town, where nobody walks. The carr-walker keeps a fire at the edge of the carr there, the only one of the fen's folk who goes in among the alders. Go down to him and see the lights for yourself. The hags hang them to draw walkers off the dry ground, and I want to know whose oil burns in them. Put down four of the hags that walk that part of the carr while you are about it.”

    Objectives

    • Kill: Marsh-Hag ×4
    • Examine: False Light (2)

    Where

    It is given by the Candlewife, in Candleholm, and it is handed in to the Carr-Walker.

    Rewards

    1005 XP at its level, 16 coin and Alder-Root Boots.

    Completion

    “Causeway oil. You can smell the tallow the lantern-tender mixes into it. So now you know where the oil goes when it is drawn off up north, some of it at least. Take these boots. You will be back in the carr.”

    In the chain

    Before it: Hold the Lantern Ring. After it: Light in Jars.
