    Varnhalt was a demigod and a king: the son of Caedmar the Far Father and a fisherwoman of Kaelmor. He was mortal, but nothing ordinary could kill him. The forge, the hearth and the shield wall were his. He was huge and bearded, with a body like a mountain and fists that broke demon armour. He fought on the front line, drank with the soldiers afterwards and knew the names of the men who died beside him.

    History

    He held the wall alone. After the war his life was the only thing keeping Ezrakhar chained and the Deep shut. Every mine, cave and crypt in Orvessa was held closed by the same power, without anyone knowing it.

    Three hundred years ago he was poisoned. In a single night a black root got into him, a root that grows only where ashblood soaks the ground, worked with a warlock's craft. It could not kill him quickly, because nothing ordinary could. It worked slowly instead. The giant in the statues became a bent, grey old man in a sickbed, and he lived with the root inside him for three centuries, until he could not bear it any longer.

    He spent those centuries in Kaelmor, the harbour town where his mother was born and where Seraveth had last stood. His vassals and human builders made the town a walled city of stone around his hall, and its people grew up with a dying king as their neighbour.

    The twin statues guard the harbour entrance. Seraveth faces the sea, and Varnhalt faces the land with his blade raised. They show both in their prime, so the first thing a new arrival sees is the giant Varnhalt once was.

    The king's last message

    The night Varnhalt died, the Order of the Fallen sealed his hall. What they are hiding is his last message: a summons to gather the best warriors in the world and stop Ezrakhar before he grows too strong. The Order keeps it quiet for two reasons. They fear panic if the city learns that Ezrakhar is rising, and they believe only the worthy should hear it. The player must prove worthy first. Being given the message is the reward at the end of the Verge main line, and stopping Ezrakhar is what the player strives for from then on.

    Description

    The last of the gods: huge, bearded, mighty even in age. For three thousand years his life held the Deep shut. He died in Kaelmor in the Ember Year, and his blade was stolen the same night. Dead. His hall is sealed.
