    Ambry: The Collector. Made in Varnhalt's forge and remembers almost nothing. Every relic of a god wakes a memory.

    Quests

    Ambry is a Forged made in Varnhalt's own forge, centuries ago. He remembers almost nothing, but every relic of the fallen gods wakes a memory in him. The line needs 40 relics the player found themselves, at least one in every zone, and trust level 3 with at least five other patrons. Every tenth relic gives a scene where Ambry remembers a piece of the old world. The last scene shows Varnhalt's final night through Ambry's eyes, including who walked out of the hall with the blade.

    • First quest: A Find from the Pillar Walk (level 10).
