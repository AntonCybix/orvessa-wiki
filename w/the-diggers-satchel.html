    The Digger's Satchel is a level 44 hidden quest of Deepway. Search the torn satchel in the rocks below the broken gates, then bring what you find to the tracker.

    Behind the level cap

    This quest is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 24 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    The quest

    The game says: “Among the rocks below the broken gates a satchel lies torn open, its strap bitten through. Whoever carried it did not get far. The flap bears the same scraped-off seal as the pay-letters.”

    Objectives

    • Examine: Torn Satchel

    It is a hidden quest: no one gives it, and it shows on no map until it is found.

    Where

    It is found by Where a Digger Fell (found, not given), in The Broken Gates, and it is handed in to the Tracker, by the second camp.

    Rewards

    4705 XP at its level and 46 coin.

    Completion

    “Where did you find this? Below the gates? Something big lives in those rocks; we hear it move at night. And look: a map of every gate on the mountain, each one numbered, and the last one circled twice.”
