    The Carters' Landing is a level 18 main quest of Port Ossery, step 6 of 9 of Port Ossery order chain. Kill four night carters at the landing on the shore north of the yard and take the marked slat from their tally post, then return to the yard foreman.

    The quest

    The Yard Foreman says: “They came up from the shore, so that is where they land. The ground runs down to the bay north of here, and a skiff is drawn up on it every night now. Put the carters there down and bring me whatever they keep their count on.”

    Objectives

    • Kill: Night Carter ×4
    • Examine: Carters' Tally Post
    • Collect: Marked Tally Slat

    Where

    It is given by the Yard Foreman, in The Ossery Roads, and it is handed in to the Yard Foreman, in The Ossery Roads.

    Rewards

    1680 XP at its level and 24 coin.

    Completion

    “The same mark on the slat as on the tags: a ring with a line cut through it. And the tally counts more crates than ever came through my yard. They go somewhere else, and I can think of only one place: the old hatch past the yard.”

    In the chain

    Before it: What the Crates Hold. After it: The Stone Hatch and Marks Under the Light.
