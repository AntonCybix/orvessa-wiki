    The Cartographers' Accord
    Organisation

    A guild of surveyors, mapmakers and pathfinders, chartered by all four powers so that no one can say its maps favour a side. In practice, the Accord is run from Ironreach money and Syndicate ports. Since the god's death the land itself has started to move, and the Accord's maps have started to lie. Its station at Sightline in Verge, run by Idris Wexley, is trying to understand why. Its best surveyor, who oversees the work from the chart office in Kaelmor, is Teodor Vey.
