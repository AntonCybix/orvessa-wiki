    Grimfur Howler is a creature of Grimbough Forest (level 37–38).

    In the game

    In the game the Grimfur Howler is level 37–38, normal. It is found in The Wolfways. It can drop rarely one of 12 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: Howlers by the Ford (behind the level cap) and The Grieving Pack (behind the level cap).
