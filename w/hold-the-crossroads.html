    Hold the Crossroads is a level 15 main quest of Milecairn, step 6 of 10 of Milecairn road chain. Stoke the watch-fire north of the crossroads, then keep the fire minder alive until the fire has caught.

    The quest

    The Night Warden says: “Since the cairns were opened, fire draws the dead. Let the watch-fire at the crossroads burn high and whatever walks this field, heath-wights and cairn-guardians, comes for it, by day as well as by night. I want it high all the same: the wagons need a light to walk toward. Stoke it, and keep the dead off my fire minder until it has caught properly. He will feed it if you stand with him.”

    Objectives

    • Examine: Watch-Fire
    • Protect the Fire Minder until the attacks end

    Where

    It is given by the Night Warden, in The Rampart Plain, and it is handed in to the Night Warden, in The Rampart Plain.

    Rewards

    1420 XP at its level, 16 coin and Watch-Fire Cloak.

    Completion

    “It has caught. They can see it from both towers now, and from the road at either end. It is the first light this crossroads has had in a month. Take this. It is warmer than it looks.”

    In the chain

    Before it: The Scholar's Request. After it: The Scholar's Cord.
