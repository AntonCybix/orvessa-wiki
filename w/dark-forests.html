    Dark forests appear in 3 zones of Orvessa; each is a different kind.

    Where they appear

    • Fellgrove (24–30): A forest that has grown wrong: twisted trunks, trees that lean towards travellers, thorns where there should be leaves. Dark because it is sick.
    • Kingsfall (32–38): A haunted forest around a fallen king's grave: mist, grey birch, barrow-mounds, lanterns no one lit. Dark because it remembers.
    • Grimbough Forest (36–42): The classic deep dark wood: black pines so dense that noon looks like dusk, wolves, witches' huts, paths that close behind you. Dark because nothing has ever let the light in.
