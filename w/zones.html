    Kinds of zone

    • Home ground: held by one power. The others pass through, but rarely quest there.
    • Contested: real provinces with people, roads and history, fought over by the powers.
    • Neutral: old lands no one holds, with free peoples, ruins and shared dangers.
    • Fault region: land broken where a god or a demon prince died.
    • Isle: sea zones reached by ship, some optional, each with its own secrets.
