    The Free Cantons
    Organisation
    Zones activeBrackwater Verge

    The self-governing villages of the Isles' marshes and deltas, mostly Tide-Born, which belong to the Syndicate on paper and to themselves in practice. They pay no harbour fees, keep their own pilots and read the water as their law. Reedstep is their voice in Verge, and Ysolde Marrow their tide-reader. The Cantons and the Accord distrust each other on principle: one measures the water, the other lives with it.
