    Hold the Lantern Ring is a level 15 main quest of Lanternfen, step 6 of 12 of Lanternfen lanterns chain. Go to the Ring's South Lantern on the island's lantern ring to start the candlewife's apprentice, then keep her alive while she fills the ring's tank there.

    The quest

    The Candlewife says: “With the oil in, my apprentice can fill the ring. Every lantern on it draws from the tank under the south lantern, and she has to pour the oil in slowly, a flask at a time. While the tank is open the whole ring burns lower than it ever does, and the dead on the south shore know it. They will come up for her. Go to the ring's south lantern and start her off there, and keep them off her until the tank is full.”

    Objectives

    • Examine: Ring's South Lantern
    • Protect the Candlewife's Apprentice until the attacks end

    Where

    It is given by the Candlewife, in Candleholm, and it is handed in to the Candlewife, in Candleholm.

    Rewards

    1420 XP at its level, 16 coin and Ring-Walker's Boots.

    Completion

    “Full, and every lantern on the ring burning high. Nothing will come up that shore tonight. Take these. They were made for walking the ring, and you have stood your ground on it.”

    In the chain

    Before it: Oil for the Island. After it: Down to the Flats and False Lights in the Carr.
