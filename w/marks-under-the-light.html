    Marks Under the Light is a level 18 hidden quest of Port Ossery. Examine the flat stone under the light, then tell the harbour clerk on the quay.

    The quest

    The game says: “A flat stone lies in the grass on the light's landward side, and if you kneel by it you will find something cut faint into it, too fine to see from standing: the same mark as on the carters' tally post. Whoever cut it here wanted it found from the water.”

    Objectives

    • Examine: Flat Stone
    • Talk to the Harbour Clerk

    It is a hidden quest: no one gives it, and it shows on no map until it is found.

    Where

    It is found by Marks Under the Light (found, not given), in The Ossery Roads, and it is handed in to the Harbour Clerk, by the quay.

    Rewards

    1345 XP at its level and 18 coin.

    Completion

    “That mark, under the light? Then somebody has been bringing the boats in by it. There is no ship in my berth book for the nights the carters landed. From now on I will watch the point myself.”
