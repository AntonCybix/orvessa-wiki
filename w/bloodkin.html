    An old, elegant and aristocratic people who live on the life of others, drawn by birth to blood-craft and dark magic. They say their first ancestor drank from a dying god on the last battlefield of the Burning Years, and was changed by it. They rule the Covenant's underworld from the courts of Redcourt, and they bind their enemies with curses rather than chains.
