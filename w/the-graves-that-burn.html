    The Graves That Burn is a level 22 main quest of The Ember Moors, step 1 of 5 of The Ember Moors fires chain. Walk north to the old grave-field and speak with the fire-tender on the field's west edge.

    Not yet in the game

    This quest is not yet in the game: it is built at its true level, under the game's level cap (24), but its part of the zone is held back for now. It opens when that part of the zone is switched on.

    The quest

    The Moor-Road Watch says: “See the smoke to the north? That is the old grave-field: the moor-folk's oldest field of graves, every one with a fire on it. We keep a fire on a grave so the dead stay where we laid them. Lately someone has been putting the fires out at night. Walk up there and find the fire-tender. She walks the rows and will know more than I do.”

    Objectives

    • Reach The Oldest Grave-Field

    Where

    It is given by the Moor-Road Watch, in Charheath, and it is handed in to the Fire-Tender, in The Grave-Fires.

    Rewards

    1890 XP at its level and 20 coin.

    Completion

    “The watch sent you? Then he told you the short of it. Look along the rows. Every fire you see burning, somebody lit and somebody keeps. The dark ones are why you are here.”

    In the chain

    It opens the chain. After it: The Cold Graves.
