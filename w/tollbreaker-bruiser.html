    Tollbreaker Bruiser is a creature of Milecairn (level 16–17).

    In the game

    In the game the Tollbreaker Bruiser is level 16–17, normal. It is found in Tollbreak Ridge. It can drop Carter's Bracers (2%), Causeway Boots (2%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.
