    The Empire began as a ring of forts in the northern highlands, built by Dwarf clans to hold the mine mouths after the Burning Years. They learned early what lies under Varkesh, and they learned to dig anyway, because godsbone is buried there and godsbone is power. Steam, rails and black powder came later. Today Ironreach is a nation of foundries and garrisons ruled from Varr, a fortress of black stone and brass raised on the foundations of a fallen god's temple. Its law is order. Its faith, insofar as it has one, is in the machine.

    History

    The Dwarves keep their own home in the Dwarves' Mountain, their old hall-city in the highlands, but it is a home, not a capital: Varr rules them as it rules everyone. Ironreach holds its Dwarves, its Goblins and its Forged under one banner and one rank ladder, and it is proud that a Goblin can become a general. It sees the Wildwood as savages sitting on timber and ore, and the Covenant as a useful supplier. Since Varnhalt's death it has said loudly that the Wildwood killed him, which is convenient, because the border forests are full of godsbone.

    What it wants from the god: the blade, and his bones, to drive its engines and arm its guns.
