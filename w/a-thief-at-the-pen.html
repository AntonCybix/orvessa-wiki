    A Thief at the Pen is a level 12 chain quest of The Kindling Hills. Tell the shearer what to do with the boy caught at the pen.

    The quest

    The Shearer says: “I caught a boy at my pen gate last night with a knife in the lashings: one of the raiders' own, from the tents. He is no older than my lad, and he cried half the night. You have seen those tents, so you tell me. Do I send him to the fire-warden in the west hamlet, whose fire his people put out, or keep him here and put him to work for every ewe they took?”

    Objectives

    • Decide with the Shearer: Send him to the fire-warden in the west hamlet. Or: Keep him at the fold and put him to work.

    Where

    It is given by the Shearer, by the fold, and it is handed in to the Shearer, by the fold.

    Rewards

    1165 XP at its level and 10 coin.

    Completion

    “So be it. I will see it done before dark, and I will not have to lie awake deciding.”
