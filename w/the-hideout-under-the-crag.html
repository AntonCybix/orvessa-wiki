    The Hideout under the Crag is a level 17 camp quest of Milecairn, step 6 of 6 of Milecairn tollbreakers chain (one of 2 branches at that step: a player takes only one). Kill the Tollbreakers' Leader, 2 Tollbreaker Veterans and 2 Tollbreaker Stone-Slingers at the hideout south-east of the ridge watcher, and return to the ridge watcher. Recommended for 3 players.

    The quest

    The Ridge Watcher says: “Then we answer him with steel. Their hideout sits under the crag south-east of here: two veterans, two stone-slingers and the leader himself, every one of them better than the Tollbreakers on the road. Do not go alone. Take two or more with you, and take them all, the leader last if you can.”

    Objectives

    • Kill: Tollbreakers' Leader
    • Kill: Tollbreaker Veteran ×2
    • Kill: Tollbreaker Stone-Slinger ×2

    It is a group quest, recommended for 3: its foes are elites.

    Where

    It is given by the Ridge Watcher, in Tollbreak Ridge, and it is handed in to the Ridge Watcher, in Tollbreak Ridge. It is offered only to a hero who chose “Refuse his terms and break the hideout under the crag” in The Leader's Word.

    Rewards

    2125 XP at its level, 50 coin, Salt Poultice ×3 and a choice of Hideout Breaker's Belt or Crag Climber's Gloves.

    Completion

    “Broken, then. Their leader went down saying what he sent by his runner: they never touched the cairns. I have no reason to believe him and no reason not to. The wagons will be safer either way. Take your pick of what they hoarded.”

    In the chain

    Before it: The Leader's Word. It ends the chain.
