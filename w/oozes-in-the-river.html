    Oozes in the River is a level 42 side quest of Deepway. Kill 8 resin oozes along the river.

    Behind the level cap

    This quest is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 24 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    The quest

    The Water-Bearer says: “He holds up a skin and turns it over: a thick dark smear runs down the inside. The signer says: "Things like tar come down the river from the failing gates. They crawl into the pools and spoil them. Eight of them fewer, and the water runs clean again for a while.”

    Objectives

    • Kill: Resin Ooze ×8

    Where

    It is given by the Water-Bearer, in The Deepfolk Caves, and it is handed in to the Water-Bearer, in The Deepfolk Caves.

    Rewards

    3000 XP at its level and 40 coin.

    Completion

    “He fills a skin from a fresh bucket, smells it, and passes it to you first. The signer says: "The first water is always for the one who cleaned it.”
