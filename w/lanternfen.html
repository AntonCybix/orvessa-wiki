    Fen villages that light lanterns for the dead, so that every soul can find its way: the Covenant's heartland, cosy in its own dark way. Lanterns are now going out across the fen, and the dead who should rest are wandering. The fen-folk blame the Sedge Sisters, a coven of fen-witches who steal lantern-light to make themselves young. They are guilty, but someone is buying the stolen light from them: Lantern-Baron Edric Vane of Tallowmere, who needs it for the Covenant's secret army of bound dead. Big-threat thread: the Covenant's army, first seen in Verge's emptied graves.

    Overview

    Marsh villages where every house hangs a lantern for the dead, so they can find their way home. Lanternfen is the Covenant at peace: fishing, reed-cutting, singing for the departed. It is gentle in its dark way, and its people are kind to strangers. The lanterns have started to burn green, which no one in Lanternfen has ever seen before.

    Rule

    Lantern-Baron Edric Vane holds the fen from Tallowmere, a town of a thousand lanterns. Covenant titles: Lantern-Baron, Lantern-Lord in Duskholm.

    Peoples Risen, Bloodkin, Blight-Born fen-folk; a few living humans
    Faith Lantern-rites: a light for each of the dead, tended for a year and a day
    Age Lanterns have burned here for six hundred years
    Biome Fen edge, lantern-fen, carr, fen meadow, salt marsh

    People

    • Candlewife Orla Wick: makes the lanterns; patron line A Light for Each.
    • Lantern-Baron Edric Vane: gracious host and secret buyer of stolen souls.
    • Old Brann the Eel-man: knows the fen better than anyone.

    Enemies

    The Sedge Sisters and their will-o-lights, wandering dead, fen-ghouls, marsh-hags, giant eels, bog-adders, mire-toads.

    Crowded places: the drowned village in the Saltings; the Sisters' stilt-huts in Carrwood.

    Elites Rares
    Sister Hagatha · the Baron's lantern-collector The Grey Lantern, a will-o-light that leads players astray (14) · Old Slick, a giant eel (16) · Lady Wick, a ghost who still carries her lantern (17)

    Dungeon

    Sedgemere (levels 16–18): The Sisters' sunken home under the black pool, full of stolen lantern-light in jars.

    Travel

    The Duskholm Road (a coach line) runs from Tallowmere to the crossroads at the Three Kettles in The Kindling Hills and back: 6 coaches on one road, a departure from each stand every 11:30, about 21 minutes on the road. The fare, 28 coin, is paid to the coachman at the stand by any hero who has already found the far stand. The ride is driven the whole way: the hero waits by the road, climbs aboard the coach while it rests at the stand, sits on it along the road and is set down beside the far stand.

    The coast boat sails from the fen's coast jetty to the coast harbour's jetty in The Shrouded Coast and back: two ships on one route, a departure from each quay every 29:21, about 29 minutes at sea. The fare, 13 coin, is taken aboard once she is clear of the pier, from heroes of level 18 or more; anyone else is set back on the pier and pays nothing. The voyage is sailed the whole way: the hero walks aboard at the pier while she rests there, stands on her deck at sea and walks off at the far quay.
