    Dead-Wood Wolf is a creature of The Shrouded Coast (level 20–22).

    In the game

    In the game the Dead-Wood Wolf is level 20–22, normal. It is found in The Grey Dunes and The Wreck Strand. It can drop rarely one of 12 rare items.
