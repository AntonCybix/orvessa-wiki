    Fuel for the West Fire is a level 10 main quest of The Kindling Hills, step 1 of 9 of The Kindling Hills fires chain. Take the bundle of fuel to the fire-warden in the west hamlet.

    The quest

    The Roadside Inn Host says: “Every farm in the hills keeps a watchfire, and the west hamlet's has been burning low. The fire-warden there sent word she is short of fuel. Take her this bundle; the track west from the crossroads runs straight to her green.”

    Objectives

    • Bring the Bundle of Fuel to the Fire-Warden

    Where

    It is given by the Roadside Inn Host, in The Three Kettles, and it is handed in to the Fire-Warden, in Tallowcote.

    Rewards

    990 XP at its level and 10 coin.

    Completion

    “From the inn? Bless that host. One bundle will see the fire through tonight. After that we will need more.”

    In the chain

    It opens the chain. After it: Gorse for the Fire.
