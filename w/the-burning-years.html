    The Burning Years
    Event
    AgeThe Second Age

    The demons came up from the Deep through a wound in the heart of Edrath. They came in such numbers that the sky over the south was red for a generation. What followed was not one war but a hundred, fought over lifetimes, and the gods fell one by one. Where a god died the land broke, and those places are broken still: the glass sands of Siltglass, the shadow-fens of Vesperfen, the lightning groves of Storm Orchard, the hour-sick Pale Reach.

    History

    The Age ended at the Last Shore. Seraveth the Tidemother held the coast with her whole sea at her back while Varnhalt went down into the Deep. She fell there, and her sea fell with her, pouring into the wound until the bay ran dry. That dry bay is Underwake. Far below, Varnhalt found the last demon lord, Ezrakhar, and could not kill him. He bound him in chains instead, and tied the chains to his own life.
