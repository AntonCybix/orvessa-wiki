    Factions overview

    How the four powers of Orvessa stand with one another.

    Ironreach and the Wildwood are enemies. The Covenant stands between them with aims of its own, and the Syndicate are nomads of the sea.

    Stances and relations

    Faction Stance
    Ironreach Empire Expands with machines and demands order. Sees the Wildwood as an obstacle and the Covenant as a useful supplier
    Ashen Covenant Trades with both sides. Its own aim is the dead of Edrath's battlefields, which makes it a threat to everyone in a different way
    Wildwood Confederacy Defends the forests and the leylines. At war with the empire; scorns the Covenant but does not fight it
    Syndicate Lives on the seas. Welcome in every port and at home nowhere but the Isles
    Pair Relationship In the game
    Ironreach – Wildwood War Guards attack each other; most contested zones lie where they meet; they cannot group together
    Ironreach – Covenant Trade and mistrust Neutral guards, full service
    Wildwood – Covenant Cold peace Guards do not attack, but traders and inns refuse service: you can pass but not stay
    Syndicate – everyone Trade Neutral everywhere. Friction with Ironreach at sea (privateers in Wreckwater Sound), but no war

    The Syndicate's two capitals. Kaelmor is the fixed capital, seat of trade and council. The Thousand Keels, hundreds of ships lashed together, is where the Syndicate truly lives: it moves to a timetable, a week in Kaelmor, a week at Port Ossery, a week at Blackwharf and so on, and brings traders, trainers and its own black market with it.

    Calm for everyone. Every faction has at least one friendly zone in every level band. About one zone in six is contested, so players can always choose between calm and danger.

    The continents

    Continent Where Held by Character Capital
    Varkesh North Ironreach Empire Coal, iron and rail: hard highlands, mining valleys and factory towns built over the gods' old battlefields Varr (the Dwarves' Mountain is the Dwarves' home, not a capital)
    Avelorn West Wildwood Confederacy Old forests, leylines and misty valleys. The forest remembers, and the logging on its border creeps closer Brightbough
    Edrath South-east, largest Ashen Covenant in the cursed south, contested in the north Where gods and demons fought their last battle: battlefields, ruined cities and dead that do not stay buried. At its centre Ezrakhar lies chained in the Deep Duskholm
    The Shattered Isles The inner sea Syndicate A chain of free ports, smugglers' sounds and reefs. Brackwater Verge is the largest island Kaelmor and the Thousand Keels

    How they stand with one another

    Relation In the world
    Ironreach – Wildwood War Guards attack on sight. Most contested zones lie where they meet. They cannot group.
    Ironreach – Covenant Trade and mistrust Neutral guards and full service. Officers buy ashblood weapons under the table.
    Wildwood – Covenant Cold peace No fighting, but merchants and innkeepers refuse service. You may pass, not stay.
    Syndicate – all Trade Neutral everywhere. Privateers clash with Ironreach freighters in Wreckwater Sound, but there is no war.

    The four powers

    When the gods fell, the mortal peoples were left to hold a world that had been held for them. Four powers grew out of the ruin, each from a different answer to the same question: what do we do now that no one will save us? They stand on a scale. Ironreach and Wildwood are its two ends and have been at war for three generations. The Covenant stands between them and belongs to neither. The Syndicate is off the scale, because it lives on the water.

    Scale Power Seat Answer to the question
    1 The Ironreach Empire Varr, Varkesh We will save ourselves, with iron.
    2 The Ashen Covenant Duskholm, Edrath Nothing is lost that can be raised again.
    3 The Wildwood Confederacy Brightbough, Avelorn The world will heal us, if we stop wounding it.
    – The Syndicate Kaelmor and the Thousand Keels Everything has a price, and we set it.
