    The Ember Year
    Event
    AgeThe Fifth Age

    It began on the night Varnhalt died. The stone sword fell from his statue, his watchfires went dark, and his blade was carried out of his hall by a hand no one saw. Every mine and cave in Orvessa drew breath. Those who carry his last spark call this the Ember Year, and they do not yet know how it ends.
