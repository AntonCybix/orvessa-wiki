    Varnhalt forged his blade during the war and poured part of his own life into it. Now that he is dead, it holds the last fraction of divine power in the world. Last Vigil is a working name.

    Description

    What it can do Kill demons, including a demon lord. Burn back corruption where it is planted in the ground. Give its bearer a god's strength, endurance and light.
    Its rule Anyone can lift it, but it amplifies whatever the bearer already is. A brave hero becomes a living beacon. A cruel or ambitious one becomes a monster with a god's power. Nobody knows what they really are until they hold it.
    Its cost It burns the bearer's life to fuel its power. One fight is survivable. Years of carrying it will kill anyone who is not a god.
    Where it is Stolen the night Varnhalt died; recovered in Underwake and made whole with First Vigil at the Forge of Oaths in Hollowcrown. Its trail is one of the great threads (section 4).

    The endgame. Someone has to carry Last Vigil down into the Deep and kill Ezrakhar. The blade will amplify whoever does it, and it may kill them. Who should carry it is the hardest question in the game.

    Varnhalt's blade, forged in the Burning Years and holding part of his life. Since his death it holds the last divine power in the world. Anyone can lift it, but it amplifies what its bearer already is, and it burns the bearer's life to do so. It was stolen on the night he died.
