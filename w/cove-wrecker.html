    Cove Wrecker is an elite of Port Ossery (level 18).

    In the game

    In the game the Cove Wrecker is level 18, elite (a group's fight). It is found in Blind Cove. It can drop Wrecker's Oilskin (17%) and Wreck Hook (8%).
