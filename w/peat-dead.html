    Peat-Dead is a creature of The Ember Moors (level 25–26).

    In the game

    In the game the Peat-Dead is level 25–26, normal. It is undead. It is found in Peatdeep. It can drop Heather-Bound Bracers (1%), Peat-Cutter's Boots (1%), Peat-Smoked Cloak (1%), Smoke-Dark Sash (1%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: Up from the Peat (behind the level cap).
