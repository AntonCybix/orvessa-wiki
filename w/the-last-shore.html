    The Last Shore

    Where Seraveth fell; now Underwake

    The Last Shore
    Dungeon
    ZoneUnderwake
    Levels48–52

    The hollow where Seraveth fell. Final boss: Malachar, and the Synod's ritual over the goddess's remains. Malachar carries **Last Vigil**, the blade stolen the night Varnhalt died; here the player recovers it.
