    Two Answers is a level 24 main quest of The Ember Moors, step 5 of 5 of The Ember Moors fires chain. Tell the fire-mother whether you will walk the writ with the harvester-captain or keep the fires with the village.

    Not yet in the game

    This quest is not yet in the game: it is built at its true level, under the game's level cap (24), but its part of the zone is held back for now. It opens when that part of the zone is switched on.

    The quest

    The Fire-Mother says: “The camp has its writ and we have our fires, and the hounds do not care which of us is right. Somebody has to find out what the harvesters do with the dead they take. You can walk with their captain and see it for yourself, or you can stand with us and keep the fires lit. I will not think less of you either way. Tell me which.”

    Objectives

    • Decide with the Fire-Mother: I will walk the writ with the captain and see what they do with the dead. Or: I will keep the fires with the village.

    Where

    It is given by the Fire-Mother, in Hearthstead, and it is handed in to the Fire-Mother, in Hearthstead.

    Rewards

    2345 XP at its level and 24 coin.

    Completion

    “Then that is your road. Whichever it is, come back to the fire when it is done.”

    In the chain

    Before it: Hounds at the Open Graves. After it: The Harvest Wagon and The Fire-Watch.
