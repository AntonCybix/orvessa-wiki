    Core premise

    The last god is dead, and the dark is rising.

    The last god is dead. For thousands of years Varnhalt held back the evil beneath Orvessa with his own life. The night he died, his blade was stolen from his side. Now the dark is rising out of every mine, cave and crypt, and the mortal factions are too busy scheming against each other to stop it.

    The night he died

    The game starts on the night Varnhalt dies. Every new character arrives in Kaelmor that same night.

    1. The omen. The stone sword on Varnhalt's statue cracks and falls into the lagoon. The harbour sees it before anyone hears the news.
    2. The fires go out. Varnhalt's watchfires on the three heights around Verge go dark at the same moment. They were never lighthouses; they were his.
    3. The blade is gone. When his keepers enter the hall at dawn, Varnhalt lies dead by the fire, and Last Vigil is missing from his side.
    4. The Deep opens. Without his life holding them shut, the mines, caves and crypts start to breathe. The first things crawl up within days.

    Two mysteries start at once. Who poisoned Varnhalt? And who took the blade? Everyone suspects Ezrakhar, because his chains were tied to Varnhalt's life and broke with it. But Ezrakhar lay chained at the bottom of the Deep, and no one can say how he did it. Closer to home, suspicion falls on Varnhalt's own physician, who vanished the same night. The thief and the poisoner may be the same person. Every faction suspects the others, and the truth stays hidden for a long time.

    The evil

    The evil has one master, three weapons and a growing army of mortals who serve it willingly.

    Ezrakhar, the Chained is the shadow the gods' light cast when they made the world, and the lord of the demons, bound at the bottom of the Deep. He is patient, intelligent and furious. Every chain that breaks gives him more reach into the world above. He does not want to rule Orvessa. He believes everything the gods made is a cage, the living included, and wants to burn it all so that nothing is bound any more. (Full entry in 1. Chronicle.)

    Weapon What it is What the player sees
    Spawn Lesser demons and twisted beasts crawling up from below Mines overrun, caves breathing smoke, livestock found torn apart. Early zones are plagued by it.
    The Hollowed Mortals whose souls a demon has eaten and whose body it now wears A neighbour who has not been himself since he went into the mine. Ember-red light deep in the eyes. A village can fall without a shot fired.
    Ashblood The rotten remains of dead demons, rising with the Deep Corrupted land, poisoned wells and dark power that tempts anyone who touches it.

    The Black Synod is the cult that serves Ezrakhar. Its members are found in every faction: desperate miners, ambitious nobles, scholars who think they can bargain with the Deep. They worship him in secret rites and tear down whatever is left of Varnhalt's protections. Their sign is a circle crossed by a single line: a broken chain.

    The Physician is Ezrakhar's servant in the Isles and commands his dead fleet. For centuries he was Varnhalt's physician and alchemist in Kaelmor, and a good man. He studied the root to find a cure, and the deeper he went into the poison, the more the dark taught him, until he had learned a warlock's craft from it. Whether he gave Varnhalt the root, or only fell afterwards, no one knows. He vanished the night the king died. Now he brews ashblood and root into draughts that turn the living and the dead rot-touched, and the people he takes become crews for the Blackwake. The Isles know him only as the Physician. He has taken a dark new name, which the game reveals later.

    The Blackwake are ships that sank long ago, their hulls grown over with black root and crewed by the rot-touched drowned. They come in fog and anchor off the coast, because they cannot put into a harbour, and their crews climb down ropes and wade ashore.

    The heroes and the factions

    The Sparkborn. With his last breath, Varnhalt's remaining power scattered into mortals across Orvessa. Those touched by it carry a spark: an ember-coloured mark that glows when the Deep is near. They are stronger than other mortals, they cannot be Hollowed, and their wounds close faster. Every player character is Sparkborn. Every faction wants them, and so does the Black Synod, because a Sparkborn soul is the only kind a demon cannot eat, but it can be broken. Sparkborn is a working name.

    The factions all know the Deep is rising. They still spend as much effort on each other as on the enemy below.

    Faction What it wants from Varnhalt's death Its intrigue
    Ironreach The blade and his godsbone, to power its machines and its guns Accuses Wildwood of the murder to justify pushing into its forests. Some officers quietly deal with the Black Synod for ashblood weapons.
    Covenant His body, to raise him as the first undead god Bargains with both sides. Its necromancers study ashblood, and nobody trusts them for it.
    Wildwood His remains returned to the earth, to heal the land Sabotages Ironreach mines, and claims the mines woke the Deep in the first place.
    Syndicate Whatever sells highest: relics, information, the blade itself Kaelmor was their city when he died. Every faction thinks the Syndicate knows more than it says, and it does.

    Light against dark, grey between mortals. The demons are evil without question. The factions are not good or evil; they are mortal, frightened and ambitious, and that is what makes PvP and intrigue feel earned.
