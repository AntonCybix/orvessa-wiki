    Spellbooks

    The spell and talent system shared by all twelve classes.

    All twelve classes with their full spellbooks from level 1 to 60, their three talent trees and the races that can play them. The spells, talents and capstones are your design from 2026-10-09, translated to English. The line In Orvessa is a proposal for how each class fits the new world.

    System rules

    The talent system follows the WoW Classic model.

    • Levels 1–60. The first talent point comes at level 10, for a total of 51 points at level 60.
    • Three talent trees per class, with 5 tiers per tree. Each tier requires 5 points in the tree above it.
    • A capstone requires 31 points in the same tree.
    • Points can be spent freely across the trees.
    • Every player gets the class's core spells. Talents change, strengthen or add spells.
    • Ranks: many spells have Rank 1–5, bought from class trainers.
