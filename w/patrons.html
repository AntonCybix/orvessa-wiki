    Patrons

    The nine patrons and their quest lines.

    Patrons
    Article
    Engine featuresTBD

    Nine patrons each have a line of 15–26 quests. The lines run across several zones and level bands, unlock concrete things and require quests from each other. No line runs from start to finish, and none is required.

    The patrons

    Patron Race, allegiance Base Line Levels Quests Unlocks Final reward

    Teodor Vey Dwarf, Cartographers' Accord The Accord's chart office, Kaelmor The Map That Lies 6–55 22 map layers (quest help, hidden places), survey tools, sea charts Varnhalt's compass, which points to hidden places and openings into the Deep
    Ysolde Marrow Tide-Born, the Free Cantons Reedstep Reading the Water 6–55 22 tide tables, river routes, swimming and diving Deep-breath: breathing under water for good
    Grenna Fusewick Goblin, Ironreach The workshop, Pillaryards Powder and Gears 4–60 26 weapon parts, ammunition types, charges, weapon mods Grenna's masterpiece, a sidearm any class can carry
    Morwen Ashveil Risen, Ashen Covenant The House of the Drowned, Shroud Skerry The Book of the Drowned 8–60 20 reagents, soul shards, curses, memory fragments Morwen's lantern, which shows echoes of the dead's memories in the world
    Kestrel Thornfield Beast-Touched, Wildwood The hunters' camp, the northern wood The Law of the Hunt 3–60 24 bounties on rares, traps, pelts, companion animals A tamed ridge-strider as a mount

    Ambry Forged, no faction The curiosity shop, Spirecourt The Collector 10–60 15 + requirements nothing until the end Ambry's case, a unique bag with extra slots and a display for the finds

    Every faction has at least one patron who speaks to it. The Syndicate has three, because everyone can start in its home.

    The rules

    • Trust in four steps per patron. A step needs player level, certain quests and sometimes reputation or coin spent with the patron. Each step opens a new shop tier and the next part of the line.
    • Cross-dependencies. The lines weave together. Grenna's quest 6 (Deep Ore) needs Teodor's quest 3, because you need his map of the mine. Pell's quest 4 needs Ysolde's quest 2: without the tide table you cannot find the smugglers' cove. Morwen's quest 5 needs you to have killed a rare that is one of Kestrel's bounties.
    • Conditions that make quests memorable. Do it at night, on a flood morning, in fog, wearing certain gear, or unseen. Example: drop four cut-throats with a pistol while wearing Odda's privateer coat.
    • Found it yourself. Items for collection quests must be found by the player. They cannot be bought or traded: Orvessa's version of Tarkov's "found in raid".
    • A few choices that close doors. Two or three times in the game the player must choose between two patrons. The choice is permanent and changes the reward, never whether the game can be finished. Example: give the stolen lighthouse lens to Teodor or to Ysolde.
    • The patrons stay home; the player travels. Most live in Kaelmor or Verge and send the player out to other continents. Every step of a line is a homecoming, with news, beer and a new story.

    Engine status. Four of these rules are not in the quest engine yet: trust levels, conditions such as night, fog or required gear, found-it-yourself items, and choices that close doors. They belong to the plan's steps L30–L33. Until then each line is written to work with today's objective types (kill, collect, interact, explore, talk, event), and the four features are layered on when the engine supports them.
