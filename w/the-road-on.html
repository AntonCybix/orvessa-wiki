    The Road On is a level 18 main quest of Lanternfen, step 12 of 12 of Lanternfen lanterns chain. Talk to the coachman at the coach stand at the town's west end.

    The quest

    The Lantern-Tender says: “Whoever buys the fen's light is not in the fen, and not in the town either. The coach climbs out of the fen from the stand at the town's west end and runs on to the crossroads and beyond. Ask the coachman where it goes, and who rides with heavy crates. The road on is yours, if you want it.”

    Objectives

    • Talk to the Coachman

    Where

    It is given by the Lantern-Tender, in Tallowmere, and it is complete as soon as its last objective is.

    Rewards

    1120 XP at its level, 16 coin and Salt Poultice ×2.

    Completion

    “Crates? Plenty, going up. They go wherever the fare is paid to. If you mean to follow them, the coach leaves when it leaves. Climb aboard.”

    In the chain

    Before it: The Ledger Page. It ends the chain.
