    Crows in the Crow-Trees is a level 30 side quest of Marrowfield. Kill 8 bone-crows north of the hall.

    Behind the level cap

    This quest is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 24 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    The quest

    The Provisioner says: “The bone-crows nest in the dead trees north of the hall and strip the drying racks the moment we turn our backs. Kill eight of them. The others will find somewhere else to steal from.”

    Objectives

    • Kill: Bone-Crow ×8

    Where

    It is given by the Provisioner, in The Lord's Hall, and it is handed in to the Provisioner, in The Lord's Hall.

    Rewards

    2170 XP at its level and 24 coin.

    Completion

    “There will be meat on the racks this winter after all.”
