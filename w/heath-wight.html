    Heath-wight is a creature of Milecairn.

    Locations

    • Milecairn

    In the game

    In the game the Heath-Wight is level 14–16, normal. It is undead. It is found in The Rampart Plain and The Quiet Mound, only at night. It can drop Carter's Bracers (2%), Causeway Boots (2%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.
