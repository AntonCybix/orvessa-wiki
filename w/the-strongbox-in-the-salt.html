    The Strongbox in the Salt is a level 47 hidden quest of Underwake. Open the salt-crusted strongbox under the far wreck's stern, then show the board-carver.

    Behind the level cap

    This quest is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 24 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    The quest

    The game says: “Under the far wreck's stern, where the salt has drifted deepest, the corner of a strongbox shows through the crust. It is sealed with old salt, and it has not been opened.”

    Objectives

    • Examine: Salt-Crusted Strongbox

    It is a hidden quest: no one gives it, and it shows on no map until it is found.

    Where

    It is found by Under the Far Wreck's Stern (found, not given), in The White Flats, and it is handed in to the Board-Carver, in Saltgate.

    Rewards

    5020 XP at its level and 46 coin.

    Completion

    “Nothing in it but salt and a few coins gone green? That is how the bay keeps its secrets: it keeps them so well they turn to nothing. Keep the coins. I will keep the box.”
