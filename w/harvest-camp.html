    Harvest Camp is a place in The Ember Moors: the Harvesters' camp.

    People and services

    Who Services What they say
    Camp Warden — “This camp works the moor by writ. Trade if you like, but keep out of the harvesters' way.”
    Harvester-Captain — “We have a writ from the city to lift the dead of this moor, and lift them we will. The moor-folk call it robbery. I call it orders. Some days I am less sure than I sound.”
    Quartermaster Sells Skin of Well Water, Grilled Sprat, Salted Herring, Salt Poultice and Linen Strip; repairs gear “Water, salt fish, linen and poultices, at camp prices. If your gear took a knock out on the heath, leave it with me and I will mend it.”
    Writ-Clerk — “Every grave we open is written down: where it lay, what we took, who carried it south. Paper keeps the peace, they tell me. It has not kept much of it here.”
