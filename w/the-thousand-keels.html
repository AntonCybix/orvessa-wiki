    The Thousand Keels
    Capital

    Hundreds of ships lashed together into one city, with streets of gangways, markets on deck and a council hall in the hull of an old warship. Thousand Keels follows a timetable: a week in Kaelmor, a week at Port Ossery, a week at Blackwharf, and onwards around the inner sea. It carries traders, trainers and a black market that will sell you anything. No one knows exactly how many ships it holds, because some of them do not want to be counted.
