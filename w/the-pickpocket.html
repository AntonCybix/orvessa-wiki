    The Pickpocket is a rare of Port Ossery (level 16).

    In the game

    In the game The Pickpocket is level 16, rare spawn, and attacks only when struck. It may appear in The Cliff Stair and The Ossery Roads. It can drop Pickpocket's Stiletto (always), Cinder-Plate Shield (3%), Cutthroat Silk Cloak (3%), Lodestone Staff (3%), Quarry-Iron Spaulders (3%), Ridge-Hewn Axe (3%), Scree-Stalker Cowl (3%), Seam-Crawler Cuirass (3%) and Timberline Breeches (3%).
