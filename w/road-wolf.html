    Road-Wolf is a creature of Milecairn (level 13–15).

    In the game

    In the game the Road-Wolf is level 13–15, normal. It is found in The Low Toll. It can drop Carter's Bracers (2%), Causeway Boots (2%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.
