    Bound Digger is a creature of The Ember Moors (level 23–25).

    In the game

    In the game the Bound Digger is level 23–25, normal. It is undead. It is found in The Grave-Fires, only at night. It can drop Heather-Bound Bracers (1%), Peat-Cutter's Boots (1%), Peat-Smoked Cloak (1%), Smoke-Dark Sash (1%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.
