    The Vigil
    Event
    AgeThe Third Age

    For more than three thousand years the Last Wall held. Varnhalt walked the world for the first centuries of the Vigil, lighting watchfires on the heights and sealing the deep places with his runes. Under his watch the mortal realms rose:

    History

    • The old kingdom of Edrath grew great in the north of that land and fell in a single winter, leaving Kingsfall and a south full of restless dead. From its ashes came the Ashen Covenant.
    • The smith-lords of Varkesh, who had learned the forge from Varnhalt's own hand, bound the scattered clans of the north into the Ironreach Empire.
    • The peoples of Avelorn swore the Confederacy oath beneath the trees that grew from a fallen goddess, and became the Wildwood.
    • The free harbours of the inner sea banded together for trade and for survival, and became the Syndicate.
