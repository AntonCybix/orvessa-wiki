    Avelorn

    The western continent. Home of the Wildwood Confederacy.

    Avelorn is green from shore to shore: old forest, misty dales, moors of heather and rivers that run clear over stone. In the Godwalk it was the realm of Thessa the Greenmother, who planted its first forests and gave its beasts their shapes. She fell in the Burning Years at the very heart of the land, and her body did not rot. It became Rootdeep, the forest that grew from her bones, and the shining lines of power that run through Avelorn's soil are her veins. The Wildwood call them leylines, and they would die before they let anyone cut them.

    Description

    After the war the peoples of Avelorn were three and quarrelsome: the long-lived Sylvani in the canopy, the Thornkin in the groves, and the Beast-Touched tribes in the hills. They swore the Confederacy oath under the first tree that grew from Thessa's bones, and they have kept it for three thousand years, mostly. The Confederacy has no king, only a council that meets at Brightbough and argues for months before it acts.

    Avelorn's danger comes from its edges. In the north-east the empire's loggers push into the forest with rail and saw. In the south lies the Blightmarch, the poisoned homeland of the Blight-Born, held by the Covenant in a cold peace. And in the far south-west, a demon prince's shadow still haunts the fens.
