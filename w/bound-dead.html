    Bound Dead is a creature of The Ember Moors (level 26–28).

    In the game

    In the game the Bound Dead is level 26–28, normal. It is undead. It is found in Fort Ashmarrow. It can drop Heather-Bound Bracers (1%), Peat-Cutter's Boots (1%), Peat-Smoked Cloak (1%), Smoke-Dark Sash (1%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: Ranks North of the Fort (behind the level cap), The Tors (behind the level cap) and The Overturned Cart (behind the level cap).
