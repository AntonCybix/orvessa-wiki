    Back to the Peat is a level 25 side quest of The Ember Moors, step 3 of 3 of The Ember Moors peat graves chain. Tell the old peat-cutter what to do with the preserved man.

    Behind the level cap

    This quest is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 24 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    The quest

    The Old Peat-Cutter says: “The fire-mother would put him back and light a fire on him, the way it has always been done. The camp's clerk would write him in the count and take him, the way the writ says. Both have their reasons. You found him. You choose.”

    Objectives

    • Decide with the Old Peat-Cutter: Put him back in the peat and light a fire on him. Or: Send him to the camp's clerk. The writ names such dead.

    Where

    It is given by the Old Peat-Cutter, in Peatdeep, and it is handed in to the Old Peat-Cutter, in Peatdeep.

    Rewards

    1825 XP at its level and 22 coin.

    Completion

    “Then that is what we will do. It is not an easy thing to choose for a dead man. Thank you for choosing.”

    In the chain

    Before it: Up from the Peat. It ends the chain.
