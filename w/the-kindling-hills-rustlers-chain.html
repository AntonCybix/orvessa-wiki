    The rustlers chain is a questline of The Kindling Hills: 6 quests at levels 11–14, one after the other. Its name is the game's working one (the owner names the threads).

    Step Quest Level Giver Type

    6 The Hidden Fold 14 Fold Lookout Camp, group of 3 (elite)

    Beside the chain

    Quests that open along the way but are not steps of it: A Thief at the Pen.
