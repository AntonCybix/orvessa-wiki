    Watchers South of the Camp is a level 14 chain quest of Milecairn, step 1 of 2 of Milecairn tollbreakers chain. Kill 3 Tollbreakers and 2 Tollbreaker Slingers on the downs south-west of the caravan outrider, and return to the caravan outrider.

    The quest

    The Caravan Outrider says: “Every morning the same Tollbreakers sit on the downs south-west of here and watch the camp. They count the wagons and they count us. Drive them off: three of the axemen and both of their slingers. Let them count that.”

    Objectives

    • Kill: Tollbreaker ×3
    • Kill: Tollbreaker Slinger ×2

    Where

    It is given by the Caravan Outrider, in The Low Toll, and it is handed in to the Caravan Outrider, in The Low Toll.

    Rewards

    890 XP at its level, 12 coin and Outrider's Gloves.

    Completion

    “They will be back. But now they know somebody is watching them too.”

    In the chain

    It opens the chain. After it: Spilled Cargo.
