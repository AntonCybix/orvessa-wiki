    The War of Gods and Demons
    Event
    AgeThe Second Age

    Gods once walked Orvessa among mortals. They built cities, taught crafts and fought alongside the first kings. Then the demons came up from the Deep, the burning dark beneath the world, and the war that followed nearly emptied both sides.

    History

    • It lasted generations. Whole regions of the world are still scarred by it: battlefields where nothing grows, cities sunk into the earth and mountains split in half. The fault regions (Siltglass, Vesperfen, Storm Orchard, Underwake, The Pale Reach) are where gods and demons died, and the land never recovered.
    • Almost every god fell. Their names survive in songs, oaths and ruined temples. Their bodies became relics: godsbone, the source of holy magic, mined and fought over to this day.
    • Almost every demon fell too. Their remains rotted into the earth and became ashblood, the source of dark magic.
    • Seraveth, the Tidemother, goddess of the sea and storms, fell in the last battle. She held the shore while Varnhalt went down into the Deep.
    • The last demon lord was not killed. Varnhalt was too weak to finish him, so he chained him at the bottom of the Deep and bound the chains to his own life. The demon lord's working name is Ezrakhar, the Chained.
