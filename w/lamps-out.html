    Lamps Out is a level 16 chain quest of Port Ossery, step 2 of 5 of Port Ossery gulls chain. Use the snuffing hood on four lamps of the stair, then find the wharf runner on the shoulder of the climb.

    Behind the level cap

    This quest is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 15 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    The quest

    The Wharf Matron says: “The lantern crews light the stair above the cove track every night so their people can run it. Take this hood up the climb and put out four of their lamps. My runner waits on the shoulder at the top; tell him when it is done.”

    Objectives

    • Use the Snuffing Hood at Stair Lamp (4)

    Where

    It is given by the Wharf Matron, in Gullrock, and it is handed in to the Wharf Runner, in The Cliff Stair.

    Rewards

    1510 XP at its level and 16 coin.

    Completion

    “Four of their lamps put out. They will know it was us by morning, which is the whole point.”

    In the chain

    Before it: Mend the Racks. After it: Knives on the Stair.
