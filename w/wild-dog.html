    Wild Dog is a creature of Port Ossery (level 15–17).

    In the game

    In the game the Wild Dog is level 15–17, normal. It is found in The Windward Downs and The Bleak Downs. It can drop rarely one of 12 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: Dogs on the Windward Side (behind the level cap).
