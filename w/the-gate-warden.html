    The Gate-Warden is an elite of Deepway (level 45).

    In the game

    In the game The Gate-Warden is level 45, elite (a group's fight). It is found in The Broken Gates. It can drop The Gate-Warden's Cleaver (always), Pit-Hound Mantle (25%) and Seal-Breaker Leggings (25%).
