    Candles on the Cairns is a level 14 chain quest of Lanternfen, step 3 of 4 of Lanternfen peat chain. Set fresh candles from the Bundle of Candles at the 3 Grave Cairns on the bank west of the peat-cutter, then return to the peat-cutter.

    The quest

    The Peat-Cutter says: “We did what was right by them. We raised three cairns on the bank west of my fire over the dead we turned up, the fen's way, with candles on each. But candles burn down, and the ones on those cairns will be stubs before morning. Take this bundle and set fresh candles at the foot of each cairn. A light for every one of them, or they will not lie quiet.”

    Objectives

    • Use the Bundle of Candles at Grave Cairn (3)

    Where

    It is given by the Peat-Cutter, in The Peat Banks, and it is handed in to the Peat-Cutter, in The Peat Banks.

    Rewards

    1335 XP at its level, 12 coin and Peat-Cutter's Leggings.

    Completion

    “Three fresh lights. The foreman will want to hear it, but not yet. Not all of them stayed down long enough to get a cairn.”

    In the chain

    Before it: What Was Not Peat. After it: The Cut-Out Dead.
