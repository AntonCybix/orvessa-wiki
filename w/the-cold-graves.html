    The Cold Graves is a level 22 main quest of The Ember Moors, step 2 of 5 of The Ember Moors fires chain. Fill the Basket of Peat Turf from 4 Stacks of Cut Peat by the fire-tender and lay a turf on each of 4 Cold Graves, then return to the fire-tender.

    Not yet in the game

    This quest is not yet in the game: it is built at its true level, under the game's level cap (24), but its part of the zone is held back for now. It opens when that part of the zone is switched on.

    The quest

    The Fire-Tender says: “Four fires went out in the night, and not by rain. Someone kicked the embers off the cairns. A grave without its fire is a grave anyone can open. Take this basket, fill it from the four stacks behind me, and lay a turf on each cold cairn. The turf catches from the ash if you set it right.”

    Objectives

    • Examine: Stack of Cut Peat (4)
    • Use the Basket of Peat Turf at Cold Grave (4)

    Where

    It is given by the Fire-Tender, in The Grave-Fires, and it is handed in to the Fire-Tender, in The Grave-Fires.

    Rewards

    2700 XP at its level, 22 coin and Grave-Walker's Boots.

    Completion

    “There. Smoke on all four again. Whoever put them out did it carefully, cairn by cairn, as if they meant to come back. Take these boots. The rows are rough walking.”

    In the chain

    Before it: The Graves That Burn. After it: A Copy of the Writ, Fallen Markers and The Untended Fire.
