    War-Iron is a gathering material of Milecairn.

    In the game

    In the game War-Iron is gathered from a Battle Relics node (Salvage 95, level 14) in The Rampart Plain and an Old War-Gear node (a rare find) (Salvage 100, level 16) in The Rampart Plain. Recipes use it. It is worth 16 coin.
