    Before the Flood is a level 16 chain quest of Lanternfen, step 2 of 3 of Lanternfen saltings chain. Examine 4 Stacks of Eel Traps on the flats west of the landing while the tide is low, and return to the old eel-catcher within 11 minutes.

    The quest

    The Old Eel-Catcher says: “Those lights I told you of come out at low water, and my traps lie out there with them: four stacks on the flats west of the landing. Only a low tide uncovers them, and the water comes back faster than you would think. Go out when the flats are bare, look over all four, and come back to me before the flood. You have one tide and the walk, no more.”

    Objectives

    • Examine: Stack of Eel Traps (4)

    The quest is timed: 11 minutes from the moment it is taken. Past that it fails, and the giver offers it again.

    Where

    It is given by the Old Eel-Catcher, by the stilt hamlet, and it is handed in to the Old Eel-Catcher, by the stilt hamlet.

    Rewards

    805 XP at its level, 14 coin and Tide-Runner's Belt.

    Completion

    “Cut open and emptied, every one, and the eels let go. Whoever walks the flats with a light does not want eels. They want nobody out there at low water. Here, this belt kept me dry for thirty years.”

    In the chain

    Before it: Down to the Flats. After it: The Drowned Edge.
