    The Confederacy is older than its name. The Sylvani, the Thornkin and the Beast-Touched lived in Avelorn's forests long before the Godwalk ended, and they remember when the trees were taught to speak. They bound themselves together only when Ironreach's axes reached the border woods. Brightbough, the city in the crowns of the giant trees, is its meeting place rather than its capital. Each clan rules itself, and the council in the canopy speaks for all of them only in war.

    History

    The Wildwood believe the land itself is the last thing the gods left behind, and that every mine is a wound. They say the Empire's digging woke the Deep. They may be right. They are at war with Ironreach, at cold peace with the Covenant, and they let the Syndicate trade at their coasts and not one step further inland.

    What it wants from the god: his remains returned to the earth, to heal the land.
