    In the game the Marsh-Hag is level 16–18, normal. It is found in The Alder Carr. It can drop Eel-Skin Bracers (1%), Fen-Walker's Boots (1%), Lantern-Carrier's Cloak (1%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.
