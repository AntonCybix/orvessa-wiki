    Resin Ooze is a creature of Deepway (level 41–46).

    In the game

    In the game the Resin Ooze is level 41–46, normal. It is found in The Deepfolk Caves and The Broken Gates. It can drop Ooze-Scoured Gloves (2%), Tunnel-Walker's Boots (2%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: Oozes in the River (behind the level cap) and Oozes at the Gates (behind the level cap).
