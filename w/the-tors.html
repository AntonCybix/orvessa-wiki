    The Tors is a level 27 side quest of The Ember Moors. Kill 8 Bound Dead in the east tors, then return to the moor-road watch.

    Behind the level cap

    This quest is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 24 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    The quest

    The Moor-Road Watch says: “The bound dead stand among the tors east of the old fort, where the east road climbs. Travellers coming in from that side walk straight into them. Put down eight.”

    Objectives

    • Kill: Bound Dead ×8

    Where

    It is given by the Moor-Road Watch, in Charheath, and it is handed in to the Moor-Road Watch, in Charheath.

    Rewards

    2950 XP at its level and 26 coin.

    Completion

    “Good. I will send the next carter that way with a lighter heart.”
