    The Coast Road is a level 18 side quest of The Shrouded Coast. Follow the coast road south and speak with the grave-digger by the graves in the dunes.

    The quest

    The Crossroads Warden says: “New to the coast? Then you want work, and the grave-digger out in the dunes wants hands. Follow the coast road south from here. His graves lie between the road's two bends, and he keeps a fire on the near side of them.”

    Objectives

    • Talk to the Grave-Digger

    Where

    It is given by the Crossroads Warden, in The Crossroads Post, and it is handed in to the Grave-Digger, in The Dune Graves. It is offered only to a hero who has not yet done The Opened Graves.

    Rewards

    895 XP at its level and 10 coin.

    Completion

    “The warden sent you? Good. I have more open graves than I have hands.”
