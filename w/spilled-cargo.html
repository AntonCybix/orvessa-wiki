    Spilled Cargo is a level 14 chain quest of Milecairn, step 2 of 2 of Milecairn tollbreakers chain. Take 4 Marked Cargo Bundles from the Tollbreakers on the downs south-west of the caravan outrider, and bring them to the caravan mistress at the camp.

    The quest

    The Caravan Outrider says: “That abandoned cart west of the camp was found with its crates opened, and the caravans' marked bundles gone out of them. I know where they went. The Tollbreakers on the downs south-west of here picked the cart over after it was left, and now they carry our bundles about as if they were theirs. Take four of them back and bring them to the caravan mistress at the camp. They are hers.”

    Objectives

    • Collect: Marked Cargo Bundle ×4

    Where

    It is given by the Caravan Outrider, in The Low Toll, and it is handed in to the Caravan Mistress, by the wagonrest.

    Rewards

    890 XP at its level and 12 coin.

    Completion

    “Our mark, every one. So the Tollbreakers picked the cart over. But a cart standing empty with its team gone is easy pickings. They took what was left. Somebody else stopped it first.”

    In the chain

    Before it: Watchers South of the Camp. After it: The Peddler's Bargain.
