    Wandering Dead is a creature of Lanternfen (level 13–16).

    In the game

    In the game the Wandering Dead is level 13–16, normal. It is undead. It is found in The Meres and The Lantern Causeway. It can drop Eel-Skin Bracers (1%), Fen-Walker's Boots (1%), Lantern-Carrier's Cloak (1%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.
