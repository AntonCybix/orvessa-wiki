    Ruts in the Carr is a level 17 hidden quest of Lanternfen. Examine the Cart Ruts on the east edge of the carr, then tell the carr-walker.

    The quest

    The game says: “Two pale furrows cross the east edge of the carr, fresh, a cart's width apart, and run toward the town. A cart has come this way at night, as one comes up the peat track past the sawn stump. Whoever cut the tallies there and on the ragged stake brought a cart this far.”

    Objectives

    • Examine: Cart Ruts

    It is a hidden quest: no one gives it, and it shows on no map until it is found.

    Where

    It is found by Ruts in the Carr (found, not given), in The Alder Carr, and it is handed in to the Carr-Walker.

    Rewards

    1275 XP at its level and 16 coin.

    Completion

    “A cart, in here? I saw the furrows and took them for the coach-yard's, lost in the dark. But a lost cart does not come back, and this one does. Somebody comes for the jars with a cart, and the tallies you found are his count of every load.”
