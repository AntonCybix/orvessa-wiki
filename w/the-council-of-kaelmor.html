    The council of Kaelmor

    The council of five houses that rules Kaelmor.

    The council of Kaelmor
    Organisation
    LeaderNo head at the start
    Zones activeBrackwater Verge

    Kaelmor is ruled by a council of five houses. Its head is a candidate elected from among the five, and the vote must be unanimous.

    The five houses

    Each house has a mark, so the player learns to recognise the name wherever they meet it: in the city, and among the deserters scattered across the world.

    No head at the start. While the king lived, his word was final and the council only ran the trade. Now it must elect a head unanimously, and House Lanthorne blocks every vote: Aldous will not give his voice to anyone until the king's poisoner is found. Kaelmor has no head when the game begins, every house wants the player's help, and the patrons pull in different directions. The house that demands the truth hides one of its own: Aldous's son serves the Synod. And even if Aldous gave way, the vote would still fail: the cult of the eye and crown steers House Merrowdyne through Reedstep, because it wants the council deadlocked.

    Pell Gannet and the black market have no seat, but everyone knows he buys votes.

    Tollivere The harbour and the tolls Odda Brine Tollivere. Loud, generous, with a smile that costs more than it gives. Wants to be head herself. A brass-chained tally board at the belt. Every Tollivere counts everything. Cassick Tollivere, a nervous toll clerk on Tidewharf Wenna Tollivere stole the harbour's toll books and runs a smugglers' quay in Varkesh
    Vantrecke The ship-clans of the Thousand Keels Hedrun Vantrecke. One-eyed former captain, blunt and impatient; wants to sail out and burn the Blackwake now. Votes for whoever gives him ships. Storm tallies tattooed on the hands, one for every storm survived Brannoch Vantrecke, a rigger in the Pillaryards Joss Vantrecke sold a clan ship to the Ironreach navy and serves there as a gunner
    Aurisande The Saltmarket guilds Isembard Aurisande. Polished, kind and ice-cold. Wants trade to go on, whoever rules. Gold rings on every finger. Taking one off means he is serious. Marisol Aurisande, an assessor in the Saltmarket who knows what everything costs Lisette Aurisande fled to Avelorn with the guild's debts and sells forged relics there
    Merrowdyne The Free Cantons' seat, for the Tide-Born Ondrine Merrowdyne. An elder Tide-Born; speaks slowly, seldom attends, votes as Reedstep wishes. Blue-green scales at the temples and shells in the braids Pip Merrowdyne, a canal girl who runs messages for everyone Coralie Merrowdyne dives for drowned relics for the Covenant in Edrath
    Lanthorne The oldest blood of Kaelmor: poor in coin, rich in name, keepers of the spire lamps and once of the watchfires Aldous Lanthorne. An old lamp lord in threadbare velvet, honest to the point of discomfort. Soot-black fingers and a lit taper always at hand Wren Lanthorne, a lamplighter in Spirecourt who sees everything at night Corvin Lanthorne, Aldous's own son. Missing, and truly a member of the Black Synod.
