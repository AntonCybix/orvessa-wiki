    Old Grave-Gull is a creature of The Shrouded Coast (level 20–21).

    In the game

    In the game the Old Grave-Gull is level 20–21, normal. It is found in The Dune Graves and The Tor Moor. It can drop Fog-Grey Sash (1%), Marram-Bound Bracers (1%), Salt-Stiff Cloak (1%), Shingle-Treader Boots (1%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: Gulls Over the Mounds.
