    The old families of Kaelmor
    Organisation

    Not an order, but older than any. A handful of Kaelmor households have tended the three watchfires and lit the spire's lamps every night for longer than anyone remembers. They do not know why, only that their grandparents did it and that the night feels wrong if they do not. Varnhalt never explained it to them. He did not need to.
