    Grey Dead-Wood Wolf is a creature of The Shrouded Coast (level 23).

    In the game

    In the game the Grey Dead-Wood Wolf is level 23, normal. It is found in The Wreck Strand. It can drop Fog-Grey Sash (1%), Marram-Bound Bracers (1%), Salt-Stiff Cloak (1%), Shingle-Treader Boots (1%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.
