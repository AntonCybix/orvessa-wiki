    Tollbreaker Veteran is an elite of Milecairn (level 16).

    In the game

    In the game the Tollbreaker Veteran is level 16, elite (a group's fight). It is found in Tollbreak Ridge. It can drop Ridge-Raider's Hood (13%) and Ridge-Raider's Pauldrons (13%). Its notable drops fill page 44 of the collection log.
