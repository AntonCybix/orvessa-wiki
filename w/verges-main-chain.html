    Verge's main chain
    Questline
    ZoneBrackwater Verge

    Verge holds the Syndicate's main chain for levels 1–10, the opening quests of the patron lines and about ten village quests. Everything about the zone itself (places, people, enemies, elites, rares, the dungeon) lives in its own tab. Every quest below uses only objective types the current engine supports (kill, collect, interact, explore, talk, event).

    Quest variety

    Every zone mixes quest types the way WoW does, so no two quests in a row feel the same. Rule of thumb: no more than two quests in a row with the same verb, and every zone of 15+ quests has at least one of each type the engine supports.

    Type What the player does Example Engine
    Kill Clear mobs in an area Hunt six heath dogs kill, built
    Boss / elite Kill a named elite or rare, often with a group The smugglers' enforcer on the islet kill, built
    Loot Collect items that drop from mobs or bosses only while the quest is active Ember-shards from the spawn collect (quest drops), built
    Find Pick up or search objects in the world The smugglers' tide table, an old cache interact (search, open, read), built
    Explore Reach a place and see it Look down into the sinkhole explore, built
    Use Light, plant, destroy or restore something Relight a watchfire interact (restore, plant, extinguish), built
    Defend Hold a place against waves Hold the headland fire for 45 s event, built
    Talk Visit and question people The talkative smuggler talk, built
    Gather Mine, pick herbs or chop wood from nodes Copper from the mountain for Grenna gather, planned (L30)
    Craft Make something at a forge, bench or still Forge a lantern hook, brew Sabeth's fever draught craft, planned (L30)
    Deliver Carry an item to someone across the map Take smoked fish to the packet pier deliver, planned (L30)
    Use item at Use a quest item on a specific spot Pour godsbone ash on a breathing mine shaft useItemAt, planned (L30)

    Verge's main chain already uses eight of the built types. Gathering, crafting and delivery come into the patron and village lines when L30 lands.
