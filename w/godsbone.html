    Godsbone
    Item

    What remains of a fallen god: bone, but also stone a god bled on, wood a god carved, iron a god forged. It is warm to the touch and faintly bright in the dark. Godsbone is the source of holy magic, the heart of the Order of the Fallen's relics, and the most valuable thing a merchant can sell. Every faction mines it, steals it and fights over it.
