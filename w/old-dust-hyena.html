    Old Dust-Hyena is a creature of The Shardlands (level 27–28).

    In the game

    In the game the Old Dust-Hyena is level 27–28, normal. It can drop Digger's Boots (1%), Dun-Hide Cloak (1%), Dust-Wrapped Bracers (1%), Stake-Cord Belt (1%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: Old Hyenas on the Shoulder (behind the level cap).
