    People whose homelands were poisoned by ashblood seeping up from the Deep, and who did not die. Over generations they changed: leathery hides, claws, and a body that shrugs off acid and venom. The Deep made them, and the Deep expected them to serve it. They refused. They are the Covenant's hardest soldiers and its most honest members, and the Blightmarch is still their home.
