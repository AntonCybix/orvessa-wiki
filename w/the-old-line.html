    The Old Line is a level 13 exploration quest of The Kindling Hills. Visit the first 3 old standing stones of the line on the downs above the strand and return to the strand forager.

    The quest

    The Strand Forager says: “Along the downs above the strand there is a line of old standing stones, one every few hundred paces, from up here all the way south toward the bay. Nobody knows who set them or why. Walk the first three for me, the one north of my fire, the one just west of it and the one to the south, and tell me what you make of them.”

    Objectives

    • Reach Old Standing Stone

    Where

    It is given by the Strand Forager, in The Pale Strand, and it is handed in to the Strand Forager, in The Pale Strand.

    Rewards

    1250 XP at its level and 8 coin.

    Completion

    “Cut marks on every one, worn nearly smooth, and all three set in a line as true as a drawn string. Somebody meant something by them. Whatever it was, they did not write it down where we can read it.”
