    East or South is a level 15 chain quest of Port Ossery, step 3 of 8 of Edrath road east chain. Tell the harbourmaster on the town green whether you will follow the carts east or the riders south.

    The quest

    The Harbourmaster says: “I can spare one pair of eyes, not two. East, the toll road climbs to the towers, and the carts went that way loaded. South, the coach road runs down to the fen and its lanterns, and the two riders went that way. Tell me which you will follow, and I will send word ahead that you are coming.”

    Objectives

    • Decide with the Harbourmaster: East, after the carts on the toll road. Or: South, after the riders to the fen.

    Where

    It is given by the Harbourmaster, in Ossery Town, and it is handed in to the Harbourmaster, in Ossery Town.

    Rewards

    760 XP at its level and 10 coin.

    Completion

    “Then go, and keep the coin's trail, not the coin. Whoever pays in it is not standing at the end of the road waiting for you.”

    In the chain

    Before it: The Harbour Ledgers. After it: Carts That Paid No Toll and Lanterns Out on the Fen.
