    Deserts appear in 3 zones of Orvessa; each is a different kind.

    Where they appear

    • The Anvil Steppe (30–36): A dry southern edge: cracked earth, thorn scrub, dust storms, bleached bones of old war-beasts. A desert that is still becoming one.
    • Siltglass (40–48): Sand dunes around fields of glass, where Solvane burned a demon host. The sand slowly turns to glass, and the glass sings in the wind.
    • Underwake (45–52): A salt desert on the dry seabed of the bay where Seraveth fell: white flats, stranded wrecks, coral ridges, salt-wells.
