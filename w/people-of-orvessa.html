    People of Orvessa

    The companions, adversaries and patrons who carry the stories across zones.

    Three companions and the adversaries carry the stories that cross zones. The patrons in section 3 carry the lines. All names are proposals.

    How the people are used

    • They come back. Lark, Lode and Eamon appear again and again from the mainland onwards, in new places and new roles.
    • They remember. What the player has done for a patron or companion shows in their lines later.
    • Someone is lost. Teodor Vey dies around level 40, and his line passes to his apprentice. The loss should be felt.
    • Roles stay roles. Villagers, guards and traders are still roles (the Pilot, the Field-Warden). Only those who carry a story get names.
