    Feral Downs Hound is a creature of The Kindling Hills (level 10–12).

    In the game

    In the game the Feral Downs Hound is level 10–12, normal. It is found in The Beacon Heights and The Shearing Downs. It can drop rarely one of 8 rare items.

    Quests that ask for it: Hounds on the Port Road.
