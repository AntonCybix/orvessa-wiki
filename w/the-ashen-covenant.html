    The Covenant was born on the battlefields of Edrath, where the gods and demons died in such numbers that the ground would not let its dead rest. The first Covenant were grave-keepers who learned to speak with what rose, and then to command it. In time they became a people. Duskholm, their capital, is a city of tombs, lanterns and markets where no one raises their voice.

    History

    The Covenant trades with everyone and trusts no one, least of all itself. Its necromancy draws on ashblood, the blood of the Deep, and every Covenant child is taught that this is a fire you warm your hands at and never sleep beside. Not all of them listen. Its Risen, Bloodkin and Blight-Born are the most feared and least understood peoples in Orvessa.

    What it wants from the god: his body, to raise him as the first undead god, a guardian that will never die again.
