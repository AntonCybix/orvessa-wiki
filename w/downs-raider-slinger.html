    Downs Raider Slinger is a creature of The Kindling Hills (level 12–14).

    In the game

    In the game the Downs Raider Slinger is level 12–14, normal. It is found in The Shearing Downs and Chalkback Edge. It can drop Drover's Boots (2%), Shearer's Bracers (2%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.
