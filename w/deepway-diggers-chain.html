    The diggers chain is a questline of Deepway: 4 quests at levels 41–45, one after the other. Its name is the game's working one (the owner names the threads).

    Contents

    1. Behind the level cap
    2. Quests
    3. Beside the chain

    Behind the level cap

    This questline is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 24 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    Quests

    2 Pay-Letters 42 Tracker Side, behind the level cap

    4 Letters to the Envoy 45 Tracker Side, behind the level cap

    Beside the chain

    Quests that open along the way but are not steps of it: The Digger's Satchel.
