    The Godwalk
    Event
    AgeThe First Age

    In the beginning the gods walked the land in bodies of flesh, and mortals could meet them on the road. They raised the first halls, taught the first crafts and stood godfather to the first kings. Every land of Orvessa still carries their marks: a stair cut into a mountain by a god's hand, a well that has not run dry in ten thousand years, a forest planted in a single night. No one knows how long the Godwalk lasted. The songs say it was long, and that it was good, and that it ended in fire.
