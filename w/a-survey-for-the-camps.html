    A Survey for the Camps is a level 31 main quest of The Shardlands, step 3 of 3 of The Shardlands after chain. Carry the survey of the lower galleries to the survey foreman at the dig.

    Behind the level cap

    This quest is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 24 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    The quest

    The Old Tunnel-Rat says: “I have drawn what I remember of the lower galleries. It is not a gift: the camps can pay me later, when they are done fighting about it. Carry it to the dig's survey foreman. He is the only one out there who can read a tunnel map.”

    Objectives

    • Bring the Survey of the Lower Galleries to the Survey Foreman

    Where

    It is given by the Old Tunnel-Rat, in Bonepickers' Warren, and it is handed in to the Survey Foreman, in Kell's Dig.

    Rewards

    3735 XP at its level, 36 coin and Surveyor's Long Coat.

    Completion

    “This is good work. Better than mine. Every camp will want a copy, and every camp will pretend it found the galleries itself.”

    In the chain

    Before it: The Lower Galleries. It ends the chain.
