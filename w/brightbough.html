    A city in the crowns of trees so large that a hall fits inside one bough. Bridges of living wood join the trees, lanterns of glowing sap light the walkways, and the Council Glade lies at the bottom, where the first oath was sworn under the first tree that grew from Thessa's bones. Brightbough is beautiful, and it is slow: the Council has been debating how to answer Varnhalt's death since the night it happened.

    Description

    The city in the treetops, built in and around giant trees. The Canopy Council of all three peoples rules only in war; in peace each people keeps its own.
