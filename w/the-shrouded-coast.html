    A fog coast of dunes and dead trees, with lighthouses that show the dead the way out to sea. Every Covenant soul is meant to sail from here on a corpse-ship, a burial boat set adrift. Now the corpse-ships are coming back, drifting ashore full, and the lighthouse keepers say the dead are being turned away. The truth: the Covenant's own lords have ordered the boats held offshore and quietly brought back, because the bound army needs bodies. The keepers are being silenced one by one. Big-threat thread: the Covenant's army of bound dead, now in its own heartland.

    Overview

    A fog-bound coast where the Covenant's corpse-ships come and go, carrying the dead to Duskholm. Lighthouses here show the dead the way, not the living. Wreckers and smugglers work the coves, and the Covenant tolerates them for a share. Since the god died, the corpse-ships have started arriving with more cargo than they left with.

    Rule

    Lantern-Baron Ysolt Mournhaven holds the coast from Shroudport, and she is not told everything.

    Peoples Risen, Bloodkin, Blight-Born; keepers' families
    Faith The last voyage: every soul sails west on a corpse-ship
    Age The lighthouses are 700 years old
    Biome Shroud, fog-coast, dunes, bleak moor, dead wood

    People

    • Keeper Ansel Graywater: the last keeper who still talks; patron line Keep the Lights.
    • Lantern-Baron Ysolt Mournhaven: honest, and furious when she learns.
    • Commander Varo Heskett of the Covenant guard: the order-follower.

    Enemies

    Covenant guard (in the hidden anchorage), returned dead, fog-crawlers, dune-scorpions, grave-gulls, dead-wood wolves.

    Crowded places: the beached corpse-ships on Deadwood Strand; the Holding Ground's camp.

    Elites Rares
    Commander Varo Heskett · the Holding Ground's Bonebinder The Pale Captain, a corpse who steers his own boat (22) · Duneback, a giant dune-scorpion (20)

    Dungeon

    The Holding Ground (levels 22–24): A fog-bound anchorage of chained corpse-ships and a sea-cave where the dead are bound.

    Travel

    The coast boat sails from the coast harbour's jetty to the fen's coast jetty in Lanternfen and back: two ships on one route, a departure from each quay every 29:21, about 28 minutes at sea. The fare, 13 coin, is taken aboard once she is clear of the pier, from heroes of level 18 or more; anyone else is set back on the pier and pays nothing. The voyage is sailed the whole way: the hero walks aboard at the pier while she rests there, stands on her deck at sea and walks off at the far quay.
