    The Dwarves' Mountain is the Dwarves' home, not a capital: a lone mountain with a great gate in its south face, halls, forges and treasure-vaults, and the Hall-Moot of thanes. Varr rules the Dwarves as it rules everyone, and the Mountain never lets Varr forget that it is older.
