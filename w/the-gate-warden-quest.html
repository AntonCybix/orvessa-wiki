    The Gate-Warden is a level 44 side quest of Deepway, step 4 of 4 of Deepway reseal chain. With two companions, kill the Gate-Warden at the east gate by the diggers' shaft, then return to the crew-chief.

    Behind the level cap

    This quest is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 24 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    The quest

    The Crew-Chief says: “The east gate by the diggers' shaft is open a hand's width. Something came up through it, with hounds like nothing that ever ran on this mountain, and it stands in front of the gate as if it were guarding it from our side. My crews will not go near. Take two more with you and put it down.”

    Objectives

    • Kill: The Gate-Warden

    It is a group quest, recommended for 3: its foes are elites.

    Where

    It is given by the Crew-Chief, near Runegate Camp, and it is handed in to the Crew-Chief, near Runegate Camp.

    Rewards

    5230 XP at its level, 92 coin, Salt Poultice ×3 and Warden-Breaker's Cloak.

    Completion

    “Gone? Then my crews can close that gate before anything else comes up behind it. You have seen what lies below now, a little of it. Most people never do, and they sleep better for it.”

    In the chain

    Before it: Fire to the Second Gate. It ends the chain.
