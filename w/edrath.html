    Edrath

    The south-eastern continent, largest and oldest. The Covenant holds its south; its north is anyone's.

    Edrath
    Continent
    CapitalDuskholm, for the Covenant
    FaithThe Covenant reveres no god. It honours the dead, and uses them.
    HoldersThe Ashen Covenant in the south. The north is neutral or contested.
    PeoplesThe Risen, Bloodkin and Blight-Born in the south. Every race in the north.
    Zones17 zones, from levels 1 to 60, and the capital

    Edrath is where everything began and where it will end. In the Godwalk it held the greatest cities of the gods. In the Burning Years it took the first wound: the demons broke through at its heart, and for a generation the sky over the south was red. Most of the gods who fell, fell here, and Edrath is a land of old battlefields, ruined temples and graves that do not stay closed. At its centre lies the Sealed Heart, the wound itself, where Ezrakhar hangs in Varnhalt's chains.

    Description

    In the Vigil the old kingdom of Edrath rose in the north and grew rich on godsbone. It fell in a single winter, eight hundred years ago, when a plague of the dead swept out of the south and its kings rose from their tombs. The survivors who learned to command the dead instead of fearing them became the Ashen Covenant, and the south has been theirs ever since. The north of Edrath was left to whoever could hold it: free towns, a Syndicate harbour, and fields that every faction now fights over.

    Since Varnhalt died, Edrath is where the Deep breathes hardest. The mines of the north smoke, the battlefields of the south stir, and the roads that lead down into the Sealed Heart have started to open.
