    Varnhalt lit fires on the heights during the first centuries of the Vigil, and kept them burning from wherever he was. They held back the Deep in the land around them. On Brackwater Verge there were three; the Kindling Hills are named for theirs. They all went dark when he died. Only a Sparkborn hand can wake one now.

    In Brackwater Verge

    The three watchfires. The Headland Beacon, the Flats Beacon and the Ridge Beacon were lit by Varnhalt's own hand and have been tended by the old families of Kaelmor ever since. All three went dark on the night he died. A rekindled fire burns for a while, gutters and goes out. The spire's three lamps in Kaelmor answer them.
