    The road chain is the main questline of Milecairn: 10 quests at levels 13–18, one after the other. Its name is the game's working one (the owner names the threads).

    Quests

    Step Quest Level Giver Type
    1 A Notch in the Cairn 13 Warden-Captain Main
    2 Between Two Cairns 13 Road Lieutenant Main
    3 Ask the Night Warden 14 Caravan Mistress Main
    4 Opened Cairns 15 Night Warden Main
    5 The Scholar's Request 15 Night Warden Main
    6 Hold the Crossroads 15 Night Warden Main, defend
    7 The Scholar's Cord 16 Travelling Scholar Main
    8 A Letter for the Tower 16 Travelling Scholar Main
    9 Hold the East Tower 17 Toll Warden Main, defend
    10 The Road West 18 Toll Warden Main

    Beside the chain

    Quests that open along the way but are not steps of it: The Lieutenant's Question, A Chalk Mark and Word Before Dark.
