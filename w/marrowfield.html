    Endless old battlefields, the Covenant's core land, where the dead of a hundred wars lie in grass that grows grey. Here the bound army the player has tracked since Verge stands ready: tens of thousands of bound dead, drilled and waiting. The Covenant's ruling Lantern-Lords are split. Lantern-Lord Aurelian Sable wants to use the army to make the Covenant a power no one can threaten; Lantern-Lord Ottilie Greyhame thinks it is an abomination that will turn on its makers. The player is caught in a quiet civil war, and learns why Sable is so certain: something in the deep has offered to lend his army strength. Big-threat thread: the Covenant's army reaches its peak, and the first hint that Ezrakhar is reaching for it.

    Overview

    Endless old battlefields where Morrow, Keeper of the Last Door, fell in the Burning Years. Since his death, no one guards the door between life and death, and here more than anywhere the dead do not pass on. The Covenant harvests them for its armies and its labour. Marrowfield is the Covenant's heartland and its greatest resource, and the reason every other faction fears it.

    People

    • Lantern-Lord Ottilie Greyhame: patron line Lay Them Down.
    • Lantern-Lord Aurelian Sable: commanding, persuasive, the villain.
    • Captain Solenne Grieve, the doubting Harvester from the Ember Moors, now a turncoat.

    Enemies

    Bound soldiers, bound officers, Sable's guard, bone-crows, carrion hounds, trench-crawlers, grave-moles.

    Crowded places: the Mustering Fields (endless ranks); the Ossuary Roads; the Citadel's courtyards.

    Elites Rares
    Bound General Marrek · Sable's Lantern-Guard captain The Crow-Mother (35) · Grimsoldier, a bound dead that remembers who he was (34)

    Dungeon

    The Marrow Citadel (levels 36–38): Sable's fortress, where the army's binding-stone lies.
