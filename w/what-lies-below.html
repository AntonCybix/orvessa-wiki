    What Lies Below is a level 37 main quest of Marrowfield, step 9 of 9 of Marrowfield lay them down chain. Report to the Lantern-Lord at the hall.

    Behind the level cap

    This quest is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 24 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    The quest

    The Picket-Captain says: “You have seen the gate. Take what you saw back to the Lantern-Lord at the hall. She has been waiting to hear it, and I think she already knows what it means.”

    Objectives

    • Talk to the Lantern-Lord

    Where

    It is given by the Picket-Captain, by the gate post, and it is handed in to the Lantern-Lord, in The Lord's Hall.

    Rewards

    2655 XP at its level and 38 coin.

    Completion

    “Chains, pylons, a mended wall. The citadel's Lord is not only raising an army. Something below the fields has offered him the strength to hold it, and he has said yes. I do not know what it is. I know that the hall will not take the same offer.”

    In the chain

    Before it: The Citadel's Gate. It ends the chain.
