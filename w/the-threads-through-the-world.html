    The threads through the world

    The great threads that run beneath many zones.

    Orvessa has no single main story. As in WoW Classic, each zone tells its own story with its own villains, often tied together in its dungeon. A few great threads run beneath many zones and grow clearer with level. They end in the raids (see Endgame and raids in 5. Zones).

    The threads

    Thread What the player follows Where it surfaces
    Last Vigil The blade stolen the night Varnhalt died; its unfinished sister, First Vigil Verge (stolen) → Kilnholm (First Vigil) → Underwake (Last Vigil recovered) → Hollowcrown (both made whole) → the Sealed Heart
    Ezrakhar and the Black Synod The chained shadow rising Hidden at first; his name is heard once in Verge, in the king's message, then not again until Vesperfen
    The Covenant's bound army Empty graves, stolen dead, a secret host Verge → Lanternfen → Milecairn → Port Ossery → the Shrouded Coast → the Ember Moors → Marrowfield → Redcourt → raid
    The old wyrms Primal beasts waking in the north Coldwater Reach → Whalebone Isles → Cinder Isle → the Rimeback Peaks → raid
    The war Ironreach against the Wildwood, fed by profiteers Ploughlands, Gullmouth → Oldroot Vale, the Sawline, the Shardlands

    Reveal order

    • Levels 1–10: Varnhalt is dead and the blade is stolen. The people blame the Rot on his death. Ezrakhar's name is heard once, in the king's last message.
    • Levels 10–25: local stories everywhere; the Synod's cults and the Covenant's empty graves appear without their masters' names.
    • Levels 25–35: the chains. Lonely Light shows what Varnhalt's death broke; the Shardlands show what godsbone can do.
    • Levels 35–45: Ezrakhar is named openly in Vesperfen. The Covenant's army stands ready in Marrowfield.
    • Levels 45–60: Last Vigil is recovered, the poisoner is revealed in the Pale Reach, and the blades are made whole for the descent into the Sealed Heart.
