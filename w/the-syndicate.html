    The Syndicate is not a nation. It is a compact of free ports, ship-clans, guilds and pirate houses that agreed long ago to stop killing one another and start charging everyone else. It has no mainland and wants none. Its fixed capital is Kaelmor, where Varnhalt chose to spend his last centuries. Its moving capital is the Thousand Keels, a fleet lashed together into a floating city that drifts the inner sea from season to season. Its peoples are the Corsairs, the Tide-Born and the Half-Bloods, and anyone else who can pay harbour fees.

    History

    The Syndicate is neutral everywhere and welcome in every port. That makes it the one power that hears everything. Every faction suspects the Syndicate knows more about the night Varnhalt died than it says. It does.

    What it wants from the god: whatever sells highest. Relics, secrets, the blade itself.
