    The Shattered Isles

    The inner sea and its islands. Home of the Syndicate.

    Between the three continents lies the inner sea, and in it a scattering of islands that sailors call the Shattered Isles. The songs say Seraveth broke them off the mainland with a blow of her trident in the Godwalk, so her children would always have a harbour. The sea here is warm, full of reefs and fast currents, and it belongs to whoever can sail it best.

    Description

    After the old kingdom of Edrath fell, its harbours were left without masters. Pirates, smugglers, refugees and alchemists took them, and to survive the wars of the mainland they swore a loose pact of mutual defence. That pact is the Syndicate. It has no king and no army, only captains, contracts and a council that meets aboard ship. The Syndicate trades with every faction, fights with any that try to tax it, and has made Kaelmor its fixed home and Thousand Keels its floating heart.

    Brackwater Verge and Kaelmor have their own book: Brackwater Verge
