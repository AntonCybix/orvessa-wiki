    The Road Past the Vault is a level 31 side quest of The Shardlands. Follow the south road past the vault to the plain's edge.

    Behind the level cap

    This quest is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 24 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    The quest

    The Dig-Marshal says: “The south road runs past the vault and on out of the plain, into old country where a kingdom once stood. If you are going that way, walk the road to the plain's edge and see what is waiting there.”

    Objectives

    • Reach The Road South

    Where

    It is given by the Dig-Marshal, in Kell's Dig, and it is complete as soon as its last objective is.

    Rewards

    2240 XP at its level and 30 coin.
