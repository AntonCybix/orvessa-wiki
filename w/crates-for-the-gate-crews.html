    Crates for the Gate Crews is a level 45 side quest of Deepway. Leave a crate at each of the 3 gate-crew posts at the broken gates, then return to the quartermaster.

    Behind the level cap

    This quest is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 24 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    The quest

    The Quartermaster says: “We keep three crew posts at the broken gates now, and every one of them is short of everything. The crates are loaded and labelled. Carry them up and drop one at each post. The crews will do the rest.”

    Objectives

    • Examine: Gate-Crew Post (3)

    Where

    It is given by the Quartermaster, in Runegate Camp, and it is handed in to the Quartermaster, in Runegate Camp.

    Rewards

    3740 XP at its level and 44 coin.

    Completion

    “All three posts stocked. That is the first time since we came up the road. The gate-captain will notice, even if he does not say so.”
