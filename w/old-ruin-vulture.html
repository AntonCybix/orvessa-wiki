    Old Ruin-Vulture is a creature of The Shardlands (level 28–29).

    In the game

    In the game the Old Ruin-Vulture is level 28–29, normal. It is found in The Broken Avenue and The Skull Vault. It can drop Digger's Boots (1%), Dun-Hide Cloak (1%), Dust-Wrapped Bracers (1%), Stake-Cord Belt (1%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: Vultures on the Broken Walls (behind the level cap).
