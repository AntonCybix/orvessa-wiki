    Highwayman is a creature of The Kindling Hills (level 13–15).

    In the game

    In the game the Highwayman is level 13–15, normal. It is found in Chalkback Edge. It can drop Drover's Boots (2%), Shearer's Bracers (2%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.
