    The Deep-delvers are one of the three kindreds of the Dwarves. The player chooses a kindred when creating a Dwarf; it changes the look and gives a small bonus.

    Contents

    1. Character
    2. Look
    3. Small bonus

    Character

    The kindred who once dug towards the Deep and sealed its gates behind them. Pale, quiet rune-masters, distrusted by both other kindreds for what they may have seen below.

    Look

    Pale skin and eyes, rune-marks in the beard.

    Small bonus

    Better sight in darkness.

    The bonuses are placeholders for balancing.
