    The Black Synod
    Organisation
    SignA broken chain

    A secret cult with members in every power, every port and every court. Its sign is a broken chain: Ezrakhar's chain, broken. The Synod teaches that the gods were jailers, that the Deep is not evil but imprisoned, and that whoever opens the door will be spared what comes through it. Its lower members believe this. Its higher members are Hollowed, mortals whose souls have been eaten and whose bodies are worn by demons. The Synod buys ashblood and pulls up Varnhalt's rune-stakes one by one. It is patient, and it has been patient for a long time.
