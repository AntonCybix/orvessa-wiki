    Varr stands on the plain where Aurelan the Lawgiver fell, and its foundations are the stones of his temple. The city is black basalt and brass, ringed by three walls and crowned by the Hammerhall, where the emperor sits on a throne said to be cut from the god's own altar. Smoke never quite clears from its sky. Its bells ring the shifts of the foundries, and its law courts never close. The Order of the Fallen keeps its greatest cathedral here, and argues with the emperor about who owns Aurelan's legacy.

    Description

    The empire's capital: a fortress city of black stone and brass on the foundations of a fallen god's temple. The Emperor rules from the Brass Throne; the city runs on rail, steam and paperwork.
