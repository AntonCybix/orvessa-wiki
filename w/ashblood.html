    Ashblood
    Item

    What remains of a dead demon: a black, oily rot that seeps up through the earth where demons fell. It poisons wells, twists animals and whispers to anyone who handles it. Ashblood is the source of dark magic, and it is getting stronger as the Deep rises.

    Description

    The black blood of the Deep, rising through cracks in the oldest mines and caves. It is warm to the touch and smells of struck flint. Ashblood is power: Covenant necromancers draw on it, Ironreach officers forge it into weapons, and the Black Synod buys every drop it can. It is also the Deep's way in. What drinks it changes, and what works with it long enough begins to hear it.
