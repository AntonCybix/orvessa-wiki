    Magical forests appear in 3 zones of Orvessa; each is a different kind.

    Where they appear

    • Oldroot Vale (26–32): Giant oaks from the Godwalk, so old they still hum with what the gods left behind. Wild magic: glowing sap, slow-falling leaves, beasts that grew too big. The reason Ironreach and Wildwood fight over it.
    • Rootdeep (46–52): A forest that grows downwards as much as up: roots older than the world, glowing caverns under the trees, rivers of light in the root-halls.
    • Hollowcrown (54–58): An enchanted endgame forest: silver trees, a sky that does not change, glades where time runs differently, and spirits that bargain. Beautiful, and dangerous to stay in.
