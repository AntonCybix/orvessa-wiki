    Ezrakhar

    The chained lord of the Deep, rising as his chains fail.

    Ezrakhar
    Person
    RoleLord of the Deep

    Ezrakhar was the gods' mistake. When they made the world, their light cast a shadow they did not mean to make, and the shadow woke. He is that shadow: as old as the world, born in the same moment.

    What he wants

    What he wants. Freedom. He believes everything the gods made is a cage: the world, its laws, its seasons, and the living themselves, bound in bodies that age and die. He does not want to rule what the gods built. He wants to burn it, so that nothing is bound any more.

    His power

    His power comes to the player in three layers, one after another as they level up:

    Description

    1. Corruption (early levels): he rots and twists what he touches. The Rot, rot-touch, ashblood and Blackmarrow.
    2. Shadow (middle levels): he works through shadow and darkness. Vesperfen's loosed shadows, the Shade torn from him.
    3. Whispers (late levels): he speaks to the desperate and makes them his. The Black Synod, the Physician, every servant who chose him.

    Ilvaren is an invented word: "the long night".

    Lord of the Deep, last and greatest of the demons, bound in chains under Edrath at the end of the Burning Years. He is not dead. He is waiting. The Sealed Heart

    Lord of the Deep, last and greatest of the demons. Varnhalt and Seraveth chained him at the end of the Burning Years, beneath the place now called the Sealed Heart of Edrath. He does not speak to mortals. He does not need to. The Black Synod speaks for him.

    Chained

    Chained by Varnhalt for 3,412 years with chains tied to Varnhalt's own life. The chains broke when Varnhalt died; what still holds Ezrakhar is the last of Varnhalt's runes, the old root-seals, and the anchor-chains such as the one under Lonely Light.

    The demons

    The demons are not his children. They were the gods' own servants, heralds, smiths and warriors, whom Ezrakhar whispered to and twisted in the Burning Years, as he later twisted the Oathbreaker of Hollowcrown. He is not the last demon prince but their lord: the demon princes (Ghazrimel, Vorgath, Uthrax and the rest) are the fallen servants who rose highest in his service.

    His shape

    His shape. When the player finally faces him he is huge, five to ten metres tall, a broken, deformed giant. His body is pieces of stone held together by dark matter: shadow that burns like a black glow, flames of pure dark plasma licking between the stones. More shadow and more corruption than fire, and more misshapen than any demon.

    If he goes free

    If he goes free, everything bound comes loose. The seasons stop. The dead do not stay dead. The living do not stay whole.
