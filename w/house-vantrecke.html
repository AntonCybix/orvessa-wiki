    House Vantrecke is one of the five houses on the council of Kaelmor. Its seat: The ship-clans of the Thousand Keels.

    Head

    Hedrun Vantrecke. One-eyed former captain, blunt and impatient; wants to sail out and burn the Blackwake now. Votes for whoever gives him ships.

    Mark

    Storm tallies tattooed on the hands, one for every storm survived

    In the city

    Brannoch Vantrecke, a rigger in the Pillaryards

    Deserter

    Joss Vantrecke sold a clan ship to the Ironreach navy and serves there as a gunner
