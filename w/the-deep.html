    The Deep

    The burning dark beneath the world.

    The Deep is the burning dark beneath the world. It is not a hell of the dead; the dead have their own door, or had one. The Deep is older than the gods' cities and hungrier than anything that lives in the light. Miners say it can be heard on a still night at the bottom of a shaft, like a forge breathing.

    Description

    Ezrakhar, the Chained was the greatest of the demon lords and the last one standing at the end of the Burning Years. He is patient, clever and furious, and he has had three thousand years in chains to think about what he will do when they break. He does not want to rule. He wants to finish the war and burn everything the gods ever built, starting with the people who prayed to them.

    The fallen princes. Ezrakhar had peers once. They died in the Burning Years, and like the gods, they left their marks. The Shade-Prince Vaskel fell in the fens of Avelorn, and his shadow did not die with him: that is Vesperfen. Others lie under Deepway, under the Slag Expanse and under the Greyed March, and the Black Synod is digging for them.

    Kinds of demon

    Kind What it is
    Spawn The least of the Deep: twisted beasts and crawling things that come up through tide pools, mines and wells. Many look almost like animals, which is worse.
    The Hollowed A mortal whose soul a demon has eaten, and whose body it now wears. Ember-red light deep in the eyes is the only sign, and only in the dark.
    Hellsworn Demon soldiers in black iron, the core of Ezrakhar's host. Rare above ground until the hellgates open.
    Doomguard Great demons of the old wars. A warlock can bind one, briefly and at great cost.
    Princes The demon lords' equals. All dead save Ezrakhar, though the Synod hopes otherwise.

    Wraiths are not demons. They are the souls of the dead that ashblood has caught before they could pass through Morrow's door. The Covenant can bind them; the Order of the Fallen tries to set them free.
