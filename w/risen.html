    The dead of Edrath's battlefields, raised by Covenant necromancy and given back their minds. A Risen remembers fragments of its first life, and most spend the second one trying to learn who they were. They are patient, dry-humoured and very hard to frighten. The living fear them. The Risen find this tiresome.
