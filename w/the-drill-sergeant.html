    The Drill-Sergeant is a level 26 camp quest of The Ember Moors, step 2 of 3 of The Ember Moors fort chain (one of 2 branches at that step: a player takes only one). Kill the Bound Drill-Sergeant on the drill-grounds (a group of three is recommended), then return to the fire-mother or the harvester-captain.

    Behind the level cap

    This quest is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 24 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    The quest

    The Fire-Mother says: “The bound drill-sergeant keeps the ranks on the drill-grounds. While he stands, the drill goes on. He is too strong for one; bring two more and put him down in the middle of his ranks.”

    Objectives

    • Kill: Bound Drill-Sergeant

    It is a group quest, recommended for 3: its foes are elites.

    Where

    It is given by the Fire-Mother and the Harvester-Captain, in Hearthstead, and it is handed in to the Fire-Mother and the Harvester-Captain, in Hearthstead.

    Rewards

    3160 XP at its level, 60 coin, Salt Poultice ×3 and Drill-Breaker's Pauldrons.

    Completion

    “Without him they stand where they were left until someone comes for them. That buys us days. Take these, and the poultices; you will need both if you go near that fort again.”

    In the chain

    Before it: The Drilled Dead. It ends the chain.
