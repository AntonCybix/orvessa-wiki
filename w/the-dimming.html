    The Dimming
    Event
    AgeThe Fourth Age

    Three centuries ago, in a single night, Varnhalt was poisoned by a black root worked with a warlock's craft. It could not kill him quickly, so it killed him slowly. He stopped walking, grew grey and bent, and took a hall in Kaelmor, where his mother was born and where Seraveth had last stood. His physician tended him there for three hundred years. The mortals forgot to be afraid. Mines went deeper than ever, the fault regions were picked over for relics, and in quiet cellars across the world a few began to listen to what whispered from below. That was the founding of the Black Synod, though no one knew it then.
