    The Bought Lieutenant is a level 19 camp quest of Port Ossery, step 3 of 3 of Port Ossery lanterns chain. Kill the lantern lieutenant and 4 lantern bravos at the tents west of the stair (the loft), with a group, then return to the lantern lookout.

    Behind the level cap

    This quest is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 15 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    The quest

    The Lantern Lookout says: “See the tents on the high ground west of the stair? We call that the loft, and it was ours. The lieutenant who held it for the crews took the buyers' silver, and now he holds it for the men with the carts, with four of our own bravos beside him. Do not go up there alone. Bring two or three who can hold their own, put him down and the four with him, and come back to me.”

    Objectives

    • Kill: Lantern Lieutenant
    • Kill: Lantern Bravo ×4

    It is a group quest, recommended for 3: its foes are elites.

    Where

    It is given by the Lantern Lookout, in The Cliff Stair, and it is handed in to the Lantern Lookout, in The Cliff Stair.

    Rewards

    2355 XP at its level, 47 coin, Salt Poultice ×3 and a choice of Loft-Watch Mantle or The Bought Man's Coat.

    Completion

    “All five down? Then the loft is the crews' again, and the carts will have to find another camp. The go-between will hear it from me, and he will not forget who did it.”

    In the chain

    Before it: What the Crates Hold. It ends the chain.
