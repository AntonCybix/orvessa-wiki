    The Mountain-folk are one of the three kindreds of the Dwarves. The player chooses a kindred when creating a Dwarf; it changes the look and gives a small bonus.

    Contents

    1. Character
    2. Look
    3. Small bonus

    Character

    The oldest kindred: halls, forges, treasure-vaults. Proud and bound by tradition; they serve the Empire and never forget they are older than it. Most Dwarves in Varr, on the rails and in the shipyards are Mountain-folk.

    Look

    Broad, braided beards with metal rings, forge-scarred hands.

    Small bonus

    Smithing skill bonus.

    The bonuses are placeholders for balancing.
