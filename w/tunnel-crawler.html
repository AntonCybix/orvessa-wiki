    Tunnel Crawler is a creature of Deepway (level 40–43).

    In the game

    In the game the Tunnel Crawler is level 40–43, normal. It is found in The Deepfolk Caves. It can drop rarely one of 15 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: Crawlers at the Gate (behind the level cap).
