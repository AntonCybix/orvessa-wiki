    The Clerk's Crate is a level 24 side quest of The Ember Moors. Find the Clerk's Crate on the heath north of the camp, then return to the writ-clerk.

    Not yet in the game

    This quest is not yet in the game: it is built at its true level, under the game's level cap (24), but its part of the zone is held back for now. It opens when that part of the zone is switched on.

    The quest

    The Writ-Clerk says: “A crate of my papers fell off the cart on the heath north of here, and I am not walking out among the adders for it. Find it and tell me whether the papers are dry.”

    Objectives

    • Examine: Clerk's Crate

    Where

    It is given by the Writ-Clerk, in Harvest Camp, and it is handed in to the Writ-Clerk, in Harvest Camp.

    Rewards

    2635 XP at its level and 20 coin.

    Completion

    “Dry? Good. Copies of the count, all of it. If those had been lost I would have had to write them all again by hand.”
