    What to Tell the City is a level 23 main quest of The Shrouded Coast, step 7 of 7 of The Shrouded Coast lights chain. Tell the coast's baron whether to report everything to the city or keep it on the coast for now.

    The quest

    The Coast's Baron says: “Cut ropes. A light-tender paid to look away on the nights the lamps were on the cliff. And under the shore past the spit, where nobody fishes, a mooring with more boats in it than any honest harbour needs. I answer to the city, and the city will ask me what I know. You have seen more of this than I have. What should I tell them?”

    Objectives

    • Decide with the Coast's Baron: Tell the city everything. If someone there is behind this, let them answer for it. Or: Tell the city nothing yet. Find out whose mooring it is first.

    Where

    It is given by the Coast's Baron, in Shroudport, and it is handed in to the Coast's Baron, in Shroudport.

    Rewards

    1690 XP at its level and 24 coin.

    Completion

    “So be it. Whatever comes of it, it was decided on this coast, by people who have seen the boats.”

    In the chain

    Before it: Before the Fog Lifts. After it: Towed Inland and A Ship Expected in the Fog.
