    The Dark Pool is a level 18 side quest of Lanternfen. Walk to the east bank of the dark pool in the carr north of the sisters' huts.

    The quest

    The Carr-Walker says: “North of the huts there is a pool in the alders, dark as tar, and nothing grows on its bank. The hags' lights lead that way, and I do not go near it. Somebody should see it, though, and tell the town what is there. Walk to its east bank and look. Do not go in.”

    Objectives

    • Reach The Dark Pool's Bank

    Where

    It is given by the Carr-Walker, and it is complete as soon as its last objective is.

    Rewards

    895 XP at its level, 14 coin and Salt Poultice ×2.

    Completion

    “Dark water, and not a ripple on it, though the wind is in the alders. Something under it is waiting. Whatever it is, it is no task for one person alone.”
