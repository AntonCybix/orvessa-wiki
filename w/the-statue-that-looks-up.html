    The Statue That Looks Up is a level 29 hidden quest of The Shardlands. Look at the statue that faces the sky, then tell the relic-warden.

    Behind the level cap

    This quest is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 24 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    The quest

    The game says: “One statue lies out on the flats with its face to the sky. No warden has turned it. The dust round it is undisturbed, as if nobody has dared come close.”

    Objectives

    • Examine: Statue Facing the Sky

    It is a hidden quest: no one gives it, and it shows on no map until it is found.

    Where

    It is found by The Statue That Looks Up (found, not given), in The Pale Flats, and it is handed in to the Relic-Warden, in Ashryn's Watch.

    Rewards

    3505 XP at its level and 30 coin.

    Completion

    “That one. We have tried to turn it, three times. It is always face up again by morning. Leave it be. Some things on this plain are not ours to put right.”
