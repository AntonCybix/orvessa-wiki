    The Watch on the Cache is a level 17 main quest of Lanternfen, step 10 of 12 of Lanternfen lanterns chain (one of 2 branches at that step: a player takes only one). Examine the Hags' Crates on the east edge of the carr to start the watch and keep the Carr Lookout alive there, then go to the carr-walker.

    The quest

    The Candlewife says: “The carr-walker's lad will sit up with you at the hags' crates on the east edge of the carr. Look the crates over with him and settle in. The hags will come for their jars, and the ghouls that follow their lights come with them. Keep them off the lad until whoever collects shows himself, or does not.”

    Objectives

    • Examine: Hags' Crates
    • Protect the Carr Lookout until the attacks end

    Where

    It is given by the Candlewife, in Candleholm, and it is handed in to the Carr-Walker. It is offered only to a hero who chose “Watch the hags' crates with the carr-walker's lookout, and see who comes” in Who Buys the Light.

    Rewards

    1065 XP at its level, 18 coin and Lookout's Bracers.

    Completion

    “Nobody came for the jars but the hags. Whoever collects was warned off, or watched the fight from the dark. My lad says a cart stood on the carr's edge east of the crates all night, with nobody by it. Wear these. You have earned them.”

    In the chain

    Before it: Who Buys the Light. After it: The Ledger Page and The Dark Pool.
