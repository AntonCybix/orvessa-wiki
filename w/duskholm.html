    A city of tombs, lanterns and quiet markets, built in the hollowed hills of southern Edrath. The living and the dead walk its streets side by side, and nobody raises their voice. The Covenant's ruling council meets in a mausoleum the size of a cathedral. Duskholm is beautiful in a cold way, and very orderly: the dead do not riot.

    Description

    The Covenant's capital: graves, lanterns and markets where no one raises their voice. The Lantern-Council of Lantern-Lords rules, now split between Sable's followers and Greyhame's.
