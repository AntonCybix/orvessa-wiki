    The Alpha of the Den is a level 39 main quest of Grimbough Forest, step 5 of 11 of Grimbough Forest out of the woods chain (one of 2 branches at that step: a player takes only one). Kill the Grimfur Alpha in the den north of the pack-trails (recommended for three).

    Behind the level cap

    This quest is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 24 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    The quest

    The Honey-Mother says: “Sweetheart, I am frightened. The wolves have gone mad, and their great leader keeps them in the den north of the pack-trails and sends them round my door every night. Every night! You are strong, and you have friends, I can see it. Take two of them and end him, please. The pack will scatter without him.”

    Objectives

    • Kill: Grimfur Alpha

    It is a group quest, recommended for 3: its foes are elites.

    Where

    It is given by the Honey-Mother, by the hut 5, and it is handed in to the Honey-Mother, by the hut 5. It is offered only to a hero who has not yet done The Grieving Pack.

    Rewards

    4655 XP at its level, 76 coin, Salt Poultice ×3 and Lost Mother's Shawl.

    Completion

    “Gone? Oh, you wonderful thing. Now I can sleep. You must come and see me again, my dear. I will think of some way to thank you.”

    In the chain

    Before it: The Cold Clearing. After it: Two Sets of Tracks.
