    Classes

    The twelve classes of Orvessa and where their power comes from.

    Magic in Orvessa comes from what the war of gods and demons left behind. Holy power comes from the remains of dead gods, dark power from the remains of dead demons, and the oldest magic from the world itself. Two classes use no magic at all. Every power keeps schools for its fighting arts, and Kaelmor keeps them all, because the Syndicate's peoples cover all twelve classes.

    Where the power comes from

    Source What it is Classes
    Godsbone Relics of the fallen gods, ground, carved or carried Inquisitor, healing alchemy
    Varnhalt's fire The forge and hearth fire he left in the world, now burning wilder Pyromancer, Spellblade, Runesmith
    Ashblood The rotten remains of dead demons, rising with the Deep Warlock, poisons and toxins
    The dark between The shadows the dead gods left behind Shadowdancer
    The world itself Wind, water, earth and beast; many say Seraveth's power lives in the sea Elementalist, Ranger
    Time Velin the Hourkeeper's gift, which only chronomancers still remember her name for Chronomancer
    No magic Gunpowder and steam, which need no god and no demon Gunslinger, Juggernaut

    Trainers

    Kaelmor has trainers for all twelve classes. Varr, Duskholm and Brightbough train their own powers' classes. The class quests at levels 4 and 8 send players back to their trainer.
