    The main questline of Lanternfen, 13 quests (levels 12–18).

    The lanterns chain is the main questline of Lanternfen: 13 quests at levels 12–18, in 12 steps; where a step has more than one quest, those are branches and a player takes only one of them. Its name is the game's working one (the owner names the threads).

    Quests

    2 Drawn Off 12 Punt-Man Main

    10 (or) The Watch on the Cache 17 Candlewife Main, defend
    10 (or) The Huts in the Carr 18 Candlewife Camp, group of 3 (elite)
    11 The Ledger Page 18 Carr-Walker Main
    12 The Road On 18 Lantern-Tender Main

    Beside the chain

    Quests that open along the way but are not steps of it: Furrows from the Mere, What to Tell the Island, The Ragged Stake and The Dark Pool.
