    Small, quick and brilliant, with an appetite for alchemy, clockwork and anything that goes off loudly. Goblins lived in the deep tunnels of Varkesh before the Empire, and they were the first to meet what lives below. They came up and joined the Dwarves because the Dwarves had walls. They have been the Empire's engineers and sappers ever since, and they know the Deep better than anyone in Varr is comfortable with.
