    The Leader's Word is a level 17 chain quest of Milecairn, step 5 of 6 of Milecairn tollbreakers chain. Tell the ridge watcher how to answer the Tollbreakers' leader.

    The quest

    The Ridge Watcher says: “A runner came down from the hideout under a white rag. Their leader offers terms. His people leave the wagons alone, and in return the wardens halve the toll for those who walk the road on foot, and look the other way when his people pass. He said one more thing, and he said it twice: his people did not open the cairns, and they do not go onto the field at night. I am a watcher, not a captain, and you have seen more of this road than I have. Do we carry his terms to the tower, or do we answer him the other way?”

    Objectives

    • Decide with the Ridge Watcher: Carry his terms to the toll warden at the east tower. Or: Refuse his terms and break the hideout under the crag.

    Where

    It is given by the Ridge Watcher, in Tollbreak Ridge, and it is handed in to the Ridge Watcher, in Tollbreak Ridge.

    Rewards

    850 XP at its level and 12 coin.

    Completion

    “Then that is our answer, and it is yours as much as mine. Whatever he makes of it, he will know the road has one.”

    In the chain

    Before it: The Ridge Approach. After it: Terms for the Tower and The Hideout under the Crag.
