    Old Carrion-Hound is a creature of Marrowfield (level 33–36).

    In the game

    In the game the Old Carrion-Hound is level 33–36, normal. It is found in The Crow Wood and The Marrow Citadel. It can drop Carrion-Hide Coat (2%), Old Rank Greaves (2%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: Chalk for the Crow-Trees (behind the level cap), Old Hounds of the Wood (behind the level cap) and The West Slope (behind the level cap).
