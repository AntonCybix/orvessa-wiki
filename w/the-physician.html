    The Physician: Varnhalt's physician and alchemist, once good, now a warlock taught by the dark. Ezrakhar's servant; commands the Blackwake.

    History

    Suspected of a hand in the poisoning. Behind the dead-ship raids across Verge; met at the end of the Verge dungeon (levels 8–10), where he escapes. Returns later under his dark name.

    For centuries he was Varnhalt's physician and alchemist, a good man who tended the king in his sickbed. He studied the root to find a cure, and the dark taught him more than he wanted, until he became a warlock. No one knows whether he gave the king the root or only fell afterwards, but he vanished the night Varnhalt died, and all Kaelmor suspects him.

    Now he is Ezrakhar's servant in the Isles and commands the Blackwake. He brews ashblood and root into draughts that turn the living and the dead rot-touched. Verge knows him only as the Physician; his dark new name is revealed later in the game.

    In the game

    In the game the Physician is the last boss of Under the Sink (level 10), in the hold of the largest hull. He fights with Flung Flask, Rot and Bitter Physic, doses the drowned to rise against the group, and at 20 % health drinks a draught and escapes through the hull's breach: he cannot be killed there. He leaves The Physician's Satchel behind, with his notes and his loot.
