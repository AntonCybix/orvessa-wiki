    Lanterns Out on the Fen is a level 16 chain quest of Lanternfen, step 4 of 8 of Edrath road east chain. Search the strange skiff drawn up south-west of the landing and take what you find, ask the eel-catcher at the landing what he saw it carry, then return to the old eel-catcher.

    The quest

    The Old Eel-Catcher says: “Two riders off the coach? They came down to the landing a while back, asking after boats. Since then a boat has been drawn up above the tide line south of the huts that is none of ours, and nobody comes to it by day. Look it over. Then ask my lad what he has seen it carry. He sits at the end of the planks half the night.”

    Objectives

    • Examine: Strange Skiff
    • Collect: Torn Ledger Leaf
    • Talk to the Eel-Catcher

    Where

    It is given by the Old Eel-Catcher, by the stilt hamlet, and it is handed in to the Old Eel-Catcher, by the stilt hamlet. It is offered only to a hero who chose “South, after the riders to the fen” in East or South.

    Rewards

    805 XP at its level and 12 coin.

    Completion

    “A leaf torn out of somebody's ledger, a whole boat's load written down: lanterns, glass, oil, and lantern-hooks like the ones we lay with our dead. And the lad says the boat goes out loaded at low water and turns south along the coast. Somebody is taking the fen's lights out to sea. Wherever that boat puts in, that is where your coin goes. The coast boat sails the same way from her jetty on the south-west carr shore. Ask her boatman where those boats put in.”

    In the chain

    Before it: East or South. After it: Passage on the Coast Boat.
