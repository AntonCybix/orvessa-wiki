    Varkesh

    The northern continent. Home of the Ironreach Empire.

    Varkesh is a hard land of iron hills, cold coasts and long grey skies. Its rivers run brown with mine-water, and its nights are lit by furnaces. In the Godwalk it was the realm of Aurelan the Lawgiver, and the kings of the north learned order and oath-keeping at his feet. In the Burning Years its northern front was where the demons were stopped by cold and stubbornness, and the bones of that war still lie under the snow of the Rimeback.

    Description

    When the war ended, the clans of Varkesh were scattered and starving. It was Varnhalt who found them again. He taught the smiths of the south to work iron as gods worked it, and those smiths became lords, and the lords bound the clans together by force of forge and law. That was the founding of the Ironreach Empire, more than three thousand years ago. The emperors have ruled from Varr ever since, and they have never forgotten that their power began with a god's hammer, nor that the god is now dead and his hammer belongs to no one.

    The empire holds most of Varkesh firmly. Its edges are less sure: the free clans of the Cragholds, a last old forest in the west that the Wildwood defends, a smugglers' harbour the empire pretends not to see, and two fault regions where nothing obeys an emperor.
