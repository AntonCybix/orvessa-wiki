    Gorse Hound is a creature of The Kindling Hills (level 12–14).

    In the game

    In the game the Gorse Hound is level 12–14, normal. It is found in The Shearing Downs and The Pale Strand. It can drop Drover's Boots (2%), Shearer's Bracers (2%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.
