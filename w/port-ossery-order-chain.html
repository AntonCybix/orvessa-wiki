    The order chain is the main questline of Port Ossery: 9 quests at levels 15–20, one after the other. Its name is the game's working one (the owner names the threads).

    Quests

    Step Quest Level Giver Type
    1 A Quiet War 15 Harbourmaster Main
    2 Two Crews 15 Harbourmaster Main
    3 The Same Coin 17 Lantern Runner Main
    4 What the Shed Lad Saw 17 Harbourmaster Main, escort
    5 What the Crates Hold 18 Yard Foreman Main, defend
    6 The Carters' Landing 18 Yard Foreman Main
    7 The Stone Hatch 19 Yard Foreman Main
    8 Who Leases the Yard 19 Harbourmaster Main, choice
    9 The East Road 20 Harbourmaster Main

    Beside the chain

    Quests that open along the way but are not steps of it: Marks Under the Light.
