    Edrath road east chain

    The main questline of Edrath across 5 zones, 9 quests (levels 15–24).

    The road east chain is the main questline of Edrath across The Kindling Hills, Port Ossery, Lanternfen, Milecairn and The Shrouded Coast: 9 quests at levels 15–24, in 8 steps; where a step has more than one quest, those are branches and a player takes only one of them. Its name is the game's working one (the owner names the threads).
