    Bestiary

    The creatures and foes of Orvessa, from the Deep to the shore.

    Under Orvessa lies the Deep: not a place so much as a wound, where the demons were driven at the end of the Burning Years and where Ezrakhar lies chained. For three thousand years Varnhalt's life held it shut. Now it seeps. What comes up is listed here from the least to the worst.

    Beasts of Verge

    The creatures of the Isles are ordinary animals made strange by a strange coast. Most are hungry rather than evil.

    Family Kinds and levels Where Habits Lore
    Tide-hounds Strand hound 1–3, heath hound 2–4, upland hound 8–9, ridge-rover 9–10 Lagoon shores, the heath dunes, Anchor Ridge Packs that flank; a wounded hound runs back and howls Lean grey dogs that follow the tide line for what it leaves behind. Ridge-rovers are tall enough to ride, and Kestrel Vane has tamed one.
    Marsh boars Reed boar 3–4, marsh boar 5–8 East meadows, the drowned grove, Braidmarsh Charge, then charge again Bristled and bad-tempered. They root the drowned grove for what the floods bury.
    Roadshell crabs Stone crab 1–5, cobbleback 5–6 Beaches, the flats, the lowland rim Pinch, shell up, throw cobbles Cobblebacks carry the stones of the drowned road on their backs, and the roadmaker would like them back.
    Shore birds Mud-stilt 1–4, wreck gull 3–5 Beaches, the flats Dive Wreck gulls follow the false lights, because ships that wreck mean food.
    Channel eels 5–7 Firmwater while it is water, Braidmarsh Strike from the water, coil They go to ground in the mud when the river turns firm, and come up angry.
    Thicket deer Thicket hind and stag 3–5 (neutral) Merefold's southern woods Defend the herd Shy. The Mountain Wood's stags are larger than any on the coast.
    Crag goats 5–6 (neutral) The Low Mountain, Hewnholm Butt Someone keeps goats on Hewnholm. No one lives there.
    Scree cats 5–8, in pairs Mountain ledges, the wood's edge Pounce, rake Hunt in pairs along the ledges and never cross water.
    Stone-crawlers Vein-crawler 5–7, slag-crawler 6–8, deep-crawler 8–10, peat-crawler 8–9 The mines, the west cave, Kilnholm, the fen on Shroud Skerry Short-sighted; spit grit that slows Blind burrowers that came up the mine shafts from below. The deeper the kind, the closer to the Deep it was born. Miners say crawlers can smell ashblood, and follow it.

    Beasts that are not quarry. Songbirds, gulls, hares, hens, dogs, butterflies, foxes, squirrels, ducks, herons, frogs, fireflies, bees, sheep, cows, goats and horses. The Isles are alive, and most of that life wants nothing to do with you.

    Outlaws of the Isles

    Ordinary people with ordinary tools, in bands of two to four. Every palisaded camp keeps lookouts.

    Band Kinds and levels Where How they fight
    Smugglers Cutters and carriers 5–6, boatmen 5 (heal), cove smugglers and cove lookouts 8–9 The flats, the beacon holm, Tidewait Camp folk
    Wreckers Light-riggers, hookmen, net-throwers 3–5; coastal kinds 8–10 Merefold's south and north-east coasts, Ebbholm's east coast False lights, hook-pulls, thrown nets
    Salvagers Ruin-diggers and rope-slingers 5–7; pit-diggers and pit-slingers 8–10 The lowland dig rings, the sink Ruin dust, sling stones
    Timber thieves Log thieves and lookouts 4–5, a patrolling pair The wood's edge Camp folk
    Cut-throats Cut-throats and slingers 8–10 Firmwater's bank Call the band at half health
    Poachers Snarers and fur-takers 4–7 (marsh snarers and marsh fur-takers in Braidmarsh) The east shore, north of Braidmarsh Camp folk
