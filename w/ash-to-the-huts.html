    Ash to the Huts is a level 41 main quest of Grimbough Forest, step 9 of 11 of Grimbough Forest out of the woods chain. Scatter the rekindled fire's ash on the fire-stones of 4 huts, then speak to the Bramble-Mother.

    Behind the level cap

    This quest is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 24 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    The quest

    The Forest-Runner says: “The cold fire burns again, and its ash belongs to the huts. Carry it to the fire-stones of the four huts that will take it: the Bramble-, Moss-, Rowan- and Nettle-Mothers'. The fifth hut will not get any. Then go to the Bramble-Mother. The hut-mothers have something to say to you, and to the wood.”

    Objectives

    • Use the Ash of the Rekindled Fire at Hut Fire-Stone (4)

    Where

    It is given by the Forest-Runner, by the foresters camp, and it is handed in to the Bramble-Mother, by the hut 1.

    Rewards

    4885 XP at its level and 42 coin.

    Completion

    “Four fires, and hers lit again. Eight would have been better, but some of us keep to the far wood, and one of us is not welcome. We will not judge our sister ourselves. The forest's to judge. She has gone to its heart, and that is where it will judge her.”

    In the chain

    Before it: Rekindle the Cold Fire. After it: The Stones at the Heart and Roots round the Stones.
