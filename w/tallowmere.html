    Tallowmere is the hub of Lanternfen: Lantern town; candle-makers, the Baron's house, the lantern-chapel.

    People and services

    Who Services What they say
    Chandler Sells Eel Oil, Skin of Well Water, Marsh-Mint Tea, Grilled Sprat, Salted Herring, Perch Skewer, Salt Poultice, Linen Strip, Fen-Walker's Boots and Eel-Skin Bracers “Lamp oil first, always: out on the causeway a dark lantern is worse than none.     Lantern-Tender — “Every pole on the green gets its oil before dark. The lights are for the ones under the water, so they can see the way home. You can see by them too.”
    Trades Trainer Teaches Herbalism, Mining and Logging “The fen gives three things to those who know where to look: herbs on the bog, iron in the red mud, and alder in the wet woods. I can teach you to take all three.”

    The inn

    The Inn Host keeps the inn of Tallowmere, two floors under one roof. A hero can check in here (the first time is free). The strongbox inside holds what a hero cannot carry; it is the same strongbox at every inn. Logging out inside the inn earns the rest bonus. This inn stands on the Covenant ground: a hero of another faction cannot check in here, so it can never be that hero's Homeward Rite anchor. The Homeward Rite brings a hero who checked in here back inside its door.
