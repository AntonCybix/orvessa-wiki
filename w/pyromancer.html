    Ironreach's furnace-mages and the Syndicate's ship-burners, who draw on the last of Varnhalt's fire. Since his death it burns wilder, and harder to hold.

    Spellbook

    Role: Ranged DPS (magic) · Resource: Mana + Heat (builds as you cast fire; at 100 Heat you take self-damage but gain burst) · Races: Dwarves, Goblins, Corsairs

    Commands devastating masses of fire. Burns down large groups of enemies with flames, fireballs and self-igniting curses.

    In Orvessa: pyromancers draw on the fire Varnhalt left in the world's forges and hearths. Since his death, that fire burns hotter and less obediently.

    Talent trees

    • Inferno (AoE): Wider Flames, Blast Radius, Chain Burn, Rolling Inferno. Capstone: Firestorm, a 12 s channelled firestorm.
    • Cinderlord (DoT): Lingering Embers, Slow Burn, Ash Cloud (DoTs slow the target), Heat Siphon (DoTs restore mana). Capstone: Soulfire, a DoT that deals 3× more damage the longer it ticks.
    • Heartforge (defence, utility): Warmth (passive health regeneration), Fire Shield (reflects damage), Smoldering Retreat (teleport through fire), Thermal Insulation (immune to cold slows). Capstone: Blazing Aegis, a 10 s shield that burns attackers.
