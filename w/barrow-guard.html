    Barrow-Guard is an elite of The Ember Moors (level 26).

    In the game

    In the game the Barrow-Guard is level 26, elite (a group's fight). It is undead. It is found in Peatdeep. It can drop Barrow-Guard's Mace (always), Barrow-Guard's Coat (25%) and Peat-Stained Hood (25%).
