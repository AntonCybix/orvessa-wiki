    The Highland Clans are one of the three kindreds of the Dwarves. The player chooses a kindred when creating a Dwarf; it changes the look and gives a small bonus.

    Contents

    1. Character
    2. Look
    3. Small bonus

    Character

    Free clans of the terraces and snow peaks who never bowed to the Emperor. Harder and wilder, quick to fight and slow to forgive. They scorn the Mountain-folk for having "sold themselves to Varr".

    Look

    Wind-burned skin, clan tattoos, fur and plaid.

    Small bonus

    Cold resistance.

    The bonuses are placeholders for balancing.
