    In the game

    In the game the Ruin-Vulture is level 26–28, normal. It is found in Landfall, The Pale Flats and The Broken Avenue. It can drop rarely one of 12 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: Vultures over the Spoil (behind the level cap) and Vultures on the Broken Walls (behind the level cap).
