    The Hollowed: A mortal whose soul a demon has eaten, and whose body it now wears. Ember-red light deep in the eyes is the only sign, and only in the dark.

    Description

    Mortals whose souls have been eaten from within by a demon, which then wears the body and its memories like a coat. A Hollowed knows everything its host knew, speaks with its voice and loves whom it loved, in every way that can be seen. It does not eat the same or sleep the same, and in the dark there is an ember-red light deep in its eyes. A Sparkborn cannot be Hollowed; the spark will not let the demon in. The Black Synod is led by them. No one knows how many there are.
