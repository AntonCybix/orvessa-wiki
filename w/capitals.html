    Capitals

    Each faction's races share one capital, each with its own district.

    Each faction's races share one capital, each with its own district. Ironreach is the exception: the Dwarves keep a home of their own in the Dwarves' Mountain, which is not a capital.

    The capitals

    • Kaelmor and The Thousand Keels (Syndicate)
    • Varr (Ironreach)
    • Brightbough (Wildwood)
    • Duskholm (Ashen Covenant)
