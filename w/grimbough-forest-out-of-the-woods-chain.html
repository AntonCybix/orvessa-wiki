    The out of the woods chain is the main questline of Grimbough Forest: 13 quests at levels 36–41, in 11 steps; where a step has more than one quest, those are branches and a player takes only one of them. Its name is the game's working one (the owner names the threads).

    Behind the level cap

    This questline is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 24 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.
