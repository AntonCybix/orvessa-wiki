    Relics and powers

    Godsbone, ashblood and the other powers the war left behind.

    What the war of gods and demons left behind in the world.

    Relics and powers

    Godsbone. What remains of a fallen god: bone, but also stone a god bled on, wood a god carved, iron a god forged. It is warm to the touch and faintly bright in the dark. Godsbone is the source of holy magic, the heart of the Order of the Fallen's relics, and the most valuable thing a merchant can sell. Every faction mines it, steals it and fights over it.

    Ashblood. What remains of a dead demon: a black, oily rot that seeps up through the earth where demons fell. It poisons wells, twists animals and whispers to anyone who handles it. Ashblood is the source of dark magic, and it is getting stronger as the Deep rises.

    Blackmarrow. A black root that grows only where ashblood soaks the ground. Worked with a warlock's craft, it can harm what has god's blood in it, which nothing ordinary can. It is what poisoned Varnhalt. In the hands of the Physician it also turns the living and the dead rot-touched. Working name.

    Last Vigil. Varnhalt's blade, forged in the Burning Years and holding part of his life. Since his death it holds the last divine power in the world. Anyone can lift it, but it amplifies what its bearer already is, and it burns the bearer's life to do so. It was stolen on the night he died.

    The watchfires. Varnhalt lit fires on the heights during the first centuries of the Vigil, and kept them burning from wherever he was. They held back the Deep in the land around them. On Brackwater Verge there were three; the Kindling Hills are named for theirs. They all went dark when he died. Only a Sparkborn hand can wake one now.

    Varnhalt's runes. Seals cut into doors, hatches and stones in the deep places, holding shut the ways up from the Deep. They are failing one by one. Runesmiths are the only ones who can read them.

    The spark. The last breath of a god, scattered into mortals. A Sparkborn's spark shows as an ember-coloured mark that glows when the Deep is near. It cannot be taken from them, and it keeps a demon from eating their soul.
