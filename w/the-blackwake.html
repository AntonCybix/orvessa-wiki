    The Blackwake
    Organisation
    LeaderThe Physician
    Zones activeBrackwater Verge

    Ships that sank long ago, their hulls grown over with black root and crewed by the rot-touched drowned. They come in fog and anchor off the coast, because they cannot put into a harbour, and their crews climb down ropes and wade ashore. The beaches at low tide are the most dangerous places in Verge.
