    The muster chain is the main questline of Marrowfield: 4 quests at levels 30–33, one after the other. Its name is the game's working one (the owner names the threads).

    Contents

    1. Behind the level cap
    2. Quests
    3. Beside the chain

    Behind the level cap

    This questline is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 24 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    4 The Brother in the Ranks 33 Muster-Sergeant Main, choice, behind the level cap

    Beside the chain

    Quests that open along the way but are not steps of it: The Soldier Who Remembers.
