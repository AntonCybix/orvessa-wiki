    Witch-Crow is a creature of Grimbough Forest (level 37–39).

    In the game

    In the game the Witch-Crow is level 37–39, normal. It is found in The Hearth-Huts. It can drop rarely one of 12 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: Crows at the Huts (behind the level cap), Crows on the Huts' Track (behind the level cap) and Crows over the South Huts (behind the level cap).
