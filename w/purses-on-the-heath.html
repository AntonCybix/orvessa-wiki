    Purses on the Heath is a level 18 side quest of Port Ossery. Kill 4 highwaymen east of the watch track and take back 3 highwaymen's purses, then return to the turf-cutter.

    Behind the level cap

    This quest is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 15 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    The quest

    The Turf-Cutter says: “The turf-carts go down the watch track to the port, and the highwaymen east of it take the coin off every driver on the way back. Put four of them down and bring back three of the purses they took. The drivers will know their own.”

    Objectives

    • Kill: Downs Highwayman ×4
    • Collect: Highwayman's Purse ×3

    Where

    It is given by the Turf-Cutter, in The Bleak Downs, and it is handed in to the Turf-Cutter, in The Bleak Downs.

    Rewards

    1120 XP at its level and 16 coin.

    Completion

    “Three purses, and that one is my cousin's; I would know the stitching anywhere. The carts can go down in daylight for a while, at least.”
