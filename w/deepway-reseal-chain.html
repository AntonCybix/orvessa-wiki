    The reseal chain is a questline of Deepway: 4 quests at levels 41–44, one after the other. Its name is the game's working one (the owner names the threads).

    Behind the level cap

    This questline is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 24 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.
