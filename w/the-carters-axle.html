    The Carter's Axle is a level 14 chain quest of Milecairn, step 1 of 2 of Milecairn wagons chain. Take the spare axle from under the carter's cart south of the road east of the camp, and bring it to the dwarf carter.

    The quest

    The Dwarf Carter says: “My own cart stands south of the road, east of the camp. Something spooked the team and they bolted with the traces. The cart can stay there. But the spare axle lashed under it is the only spare in this camp, and the wagoner needs it. Bring it to me.”

    Objectives

    • Examine: Carter's Cart
    • Bring the Spare Axle to the Dwarf Carter

    Where

    It is given by the Dwarf Carter, by the wagonrest cart 1, and it is handed in to the Dwarf Carter, by the wagonrest cart 1.

    Rewards

    890 XP at its level and 12 coin.

    Completion

    “Whole and sound. Good dwarven ash, that. The wagoner can stop cursing his cracked one now.”

    In the chain

    It opens the chain. After it: The Bolted Team.
