    Stakes at the Ford is a level 39 side quest of Grimbough Forest. Mark the 2 fording stakes on the banks of the ford.

    Behind the level cap

    This quest is behind the level cap: it is built at its true level, but the game's level cap is 24 for now, so it is not in the game yet. It opens when the cap is raised.

    The quest

    The Woodward says: “In the morning mist you cannot see the ford from the road, and a crew that misses it walks into deep water. There are two stakes, one on each bank, and they need fresh paint every season. Go and mark them.”

    Objectives

    • Examine: Fording Stake (2)

    Where

    It is given by the Woodward, in Thistle's End, and it is handed in to the Woodward, in Thistle's End.

    Rewards

    2795 XP at its level and 34 coin.

    Completion

    “Good. Small work, but it is the small work that keeps a village alive.”
