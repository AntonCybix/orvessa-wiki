    Bonepicker Tunnel-Rat is a creature of The Shardlands (level 28–29).

    In the game

    In the game the Bonepicker Tunnel-Rat is level 28–29, normal. It is found in Bonepickers' Warren. It can drop Digger's Boots (1%), Dun-Hide Cloak (1%), Dust-Wrapped Bracers (1%), Stake-Cord Belt (1%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: Rats under the Stakes (behind the level cap), Tunnel-Rats (behind the level cap) and The Old Rat's Price (behind the level cap).
