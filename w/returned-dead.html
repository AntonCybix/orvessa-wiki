    Returned Dead is a creature of The Shrouded Coast (level 20–22).

    In the game

    In the game the Returned Dead is level 20–22, normal. It is undead. It is found in The Wreck Strand and The Tor Moor. It can drop Fog-Grey Sash (1%), Marram-Bound Bracers (1%), Salt-Stiff Cloak (1%), Shingle-Treader Boots (1%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: The Dead on the Strand.
