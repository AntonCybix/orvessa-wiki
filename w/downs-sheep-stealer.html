    Downs Sheep-Stealer is a creature of The Kindling Hills (level 12–13).

    In the game

    In the game the Downs Sheep-Stealer is level 12–13, normal. It is found in The Shearing Downs. It can drop Drover's Boots (2%), Shearer's Bracers (2%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: The Tent Camp.
