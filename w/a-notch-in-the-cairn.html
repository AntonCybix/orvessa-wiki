    A Notch in the Cairn is a level 13 main quest of Milecairn, step 1 of 6 of Milecairn road chain. Cut a notch with the warden's chisel in the cairn by the signpost east of the tower, then speak to the road lieutenant there.

    The quest

    The Warden-Captain says: “Every warden who walks this road east cuts a notch in the first cairn past the tower. It is an old custom, and it tells the next warden the road was walked. Take this chisel and cut yours in the cairn by the signpost, east along the road. A lieutenant is waiting there for news of the road. Tell him the wardens walk it yet.”

    Objectives

    • Use the Warden's Chisel at Roadside Cairn

    Where

    It is given by the Warden-Captain, in Fourth Cairn Tower, and it is handed in to the Road Lieutenant, in The Low Toll.

    Rewards

    835 XP at its level and 10 coin.

    Completion

    “The warden-captain sent you? Good. A notch in a stone is a small thing, but it is more news than this road has given me in a week.”

    In the chain

    It opens the chain. After it: Between Two Cairns and The Lieutenant's Question.
