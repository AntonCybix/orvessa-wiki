    Dig Hireling is a creature of Underwake (level 47–50).

    In the game

    In the game the Dig Hireling is level 47–50, normal. It is found in The Last Shore. It can drop Hireling's Belt (2%), Wreck-Rope Gloves (2%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: Hirelings on the Ridges (behind the level cap) and Hirelings below the Ridges (behind the level cap).
