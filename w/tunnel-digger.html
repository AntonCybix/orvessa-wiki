    Tunnel Digger is a creature of Deepway (level 42–46).

    In the game

    In the game the Tunnel Digger is level 42–46, normal. It is found in The Deepfolk Caves, The Broken Gates and The Ashblood Falls. It can drop Dig Guard's Belt (2%), Digger's Hood (2%) and rarely one of 8 rare items.

    Quests

    Quests that ask for it: The Diggers' Tools (behind the level cap) and Diggers on the Valley Road (behind the level cap).
